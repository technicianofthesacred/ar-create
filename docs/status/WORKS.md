## Source capabilities and bounded evidence

These describe available source paths, not proof for whichever revision is installed. Installed means recorded in the running app’s release; full acceptance remains separate.

- **Library and photographs**: sources, personal notes, locations, topics and known photo usage; indexed unused photos can enter the replace/insert flow, with Undo.
- **Material intake**: microphone capture connects to the retained recording, Start and Retry owners.
- **Source**: saved transcript corrections, speaker controls, retained translation review, literal saved-passage lookup, Source to select passages to Translate.
- **Research**: notebook, point questions, cited discussion, brief preparation and explicit local Tea Atlas retrieval; saved Research choices keep their lineage.
- **Direction**: inline angle and title choices with rejection reasons, saved and Undoable in the human-direction owner; per-piece grid; choices survive a page redraw.
- **Write**: exact native owner, stable paragraph and caret recovery; sourced proposals cite exact quotations, unsupported points stay questions, and Adrian reviews a draft as a whole with a human-only Finish.
- **Read and Check**: shared reading geometry for Read and Write; independent Carousel reading; output-specific checks on exact passages.
- **Media and Arrange**: nested folders, album membership, explicit photo placement in Articles, captions, credits, composed roles, drag reorder, Move, Focus, Shape, Replace; Undo.
- **Pieces**: Article and Carousel are independent pieces under one subject; the subject board shows inert thumbnails of each; + makes another piece.
- **Audience and Share**: separate output briefs, independent linked editions, Chinese linked translation, private invitations with recipient and language sections.
- **Outputs**: Markdown and formatted HTML downloads, retained export receipts and prior editions kept distinct. Carousel PNG export exists for reviewed saved slides; a working draft or receipt does not mean publication approval.
- **Coordination**: one CLI and one MCP for agents, actor stamped on every write, leases, handoff, ask, journal and events; Home lists decisions, optional work and committed agent work.
- **Release**: the installed app is a recorded commit (`RELEASE.json`), built only by `scripts/release.mjs`, with the previous build kept for rollback.
