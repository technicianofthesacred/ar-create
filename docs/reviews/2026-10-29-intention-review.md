# AR Create — purpose and independent review guide

29 October 2026. This guide connects existing requirements to an independent second opinion. It does not replace PRODUCT, CURRENT or TODO and does not add another backlog. It contains no private manuscripts, photographs, credentials or conversation archives.

## The intended result

AR Create helps the author turn lived experience, conversations, recordings, notes, photographs and ideas into pieces they are proud to publish or use. Agents assist with understanding, research, organisation, alternatives and production. The author directs meaning, taste and final editorial judgment. Engineering should reduce the author's management burden.

The author should be able to return to the right work, see its saved understanding and decisions, make a useful creative contribution, and take away a faithful output. Sources are reusable; articles, carousels and other requested expressions keep independent wording and review states. The twelve sections are entrances to work, not compulsory gates. Any valid material or an existing project may be the starting point.

## Independent review conclusion

A fresh-context read-only review of the PR106 snapshot found that the product model fits the intention, but the experience only partially delivers it. It verified representative code, export hashes, receipts and screenshots; it did not operate the live app, inspect every section or certify human acceptance. Later releases may resolve its particular examples.

**Keep:** maintained source identities, original material, canonical writer/history/recovery, independent output words, logical albums, human editorial approval, direct editing, one integration owner and exact release evidence.

**Simplify:** selected-owner resolution and its presentation, repeated controls and titles, disclosure-heavy editing, fragmented status accounts and repeated cross-chat relays. Simplification does not mean deleting preservation safeguards or replacing storage.

**Change:** report the author capability now possible and its practical limit. Test results and releases are supporting evidence, not the main unit of progress. Keep readable working downloads distinct from faithful audience-facing editions. Saved research counts do not by themselves establish useful creative collaboration.

**Finish first:** reliable return to the exact work; actual requested exports that render faithfully; then a useful context-aware preparation/proposal/human-decision interaction on a selected piece, with the decision preserved and carried to the correct output. Broader library organisation, translations, footage and remote/offline access follow their existing requirements; they need not all precede the first satisfying local journey.

The review found no basis for a wholesale storage rewrite or blaming model choice. Preservation adapters can be justified; examine their actual contracts before proposing replacement.

## Evidence rules for the next review

Distinguish intended, source-built, isolated-tested, main-app verified, installed and human-accepted. Native human approval is not inferred from a layout choice or a request to fix engineering. An export receipt proves file identity, not visual fidelity. A successful reload does not alone resolve an intermittent startup failure.

For relevant changes, verify the selected work in the main app, actual downloaded files, same-viewport rendering, reopening and source/output continuity. Preserve original wording, protected passages, unresolved editorial marks and independent sibling outputs. No provider execution, content publication or sending follows from a review request.

## Questions for a second reviewer

1. Does the built author experience express the purpose and seven pillars? Separate observed evidence from hypotheses.
2. What should be kept, simplified, changed or stopped? Identify what should not be rebuilt.
3. Are the file/writer/approval contracts coherent, and is their complexity being hidden from the author appropriately?
4. What precise edits would make the existing purpose, status and acceptance documents clearer without adding another board or specification?
5. What is the shortest coherent finish sequence, with observable outcomes and the minimum genuine human decisions?

Request only the concrete additional evidence needed to resolve a disputed finding. Private source material is not part of this code repository; lack of access must not be presented as a verified product failure. This is a review guide, not permission to create a competing build or change editorial decisions.
