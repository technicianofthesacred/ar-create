# Version two walked as an author, 11 November 2026

Walked on the demo only (port 8854, invented data), at 390 and 1440 wide, dark theme, with a light-theme look at Home, the subject page, the slide studio and Looks. Every page was opened as `agent:claude-code`, so human-only steps (starting a research topic, saving prose in Write) were refused and could not be walked; they are listed at the end. Nothing scrolled sideways on any screen at either width. Pictures of the whole walk are on the published page that goes with this report.

## The five findings that matter most

1. **Adding material leaves version two and loses the subject.** On a new subject, "A recording", "A note or a link", "Photographs" and "Or drop anything here" all open Advanced on Material. The top bar there says "All subjects" and the address loses the subject a moment later. A file added there is attached to the subject anyway (it shows "In a subject"), and the small "Version two" link on the top line does return him to the same subject, form and step. But he is dropped into a list of 49 unrelated notes with nothing saying "this is for Dawn at the tide pools". Capture in the top bar does nothing at all.
2. **Write in Advanced does not open the piece he came from.** In a subject with an Article and a Carousel, "Write in Advanced" from the Carousel's post text and "Write" from the Article's words both land on the Article's words. In the subject with only a Carousel plan it lands on an empty Article page ("The page is empty"), and the tabs read Article and Video with no Carousel. The subject and the section are kept; the piece is not. Going back keeps the subject, form and step every time (checked three times).
3. **The subject header prints internal text.** As soon as the dish is named, the line beside the subject's name reads "# Concept ## Author thoughts, supplied verbatim ## Adopted human direction, explicit ..." and so on, cut off at the edge on desktop and five lines of it on a phone, above everything else on the page. It shows on every screen of that subject. It should show his own sentence only.
4. **A new Carousel is a dead end in version two.** Its steps say "Waits for stories to be proposed" and "Waits for the slides" with nothing he can do and no word on who proposes them. "Open the slide studio" then shows a bare page, "Not built yet. No Carousel has been laid out for this plan yet", with text touching the left edge of the screen. The studio itself is good on the demo's ready-made plan, so the gap is between making a Carousel and reaching it. An Article cannot be started in version two at all: its tile says "Write a few words first", and Write is in Advanced.
5. **Some buttons say nothing.** "New group" in Research made no group and showed no message. A refused action shows a plain line ("Only Adrian can decide this"), which is good, but the same click as a quiet failure is not. A new subject gets no research topic of its own, yet the page says topics "make themselves from what you gather". The sixteen demo topics each show a title ("The road north: research") and, beside it, a line repeating it ("From the research for The road north").

## The journey, step by step

**1. Home.** Clear. Needs you sits first, with the answer buttons on the same row as each question; On your desk follows; Everything lists by folder. He can tell what needs him in one look. The empty boxes at the top of each desk card are where photographs will go and look like a fault. "New subject" opens a single field, "What is it about?"; pressing Enter creates the subject and opens its own page with its name on top. That is the old U1 closed. Would not stop him.

**2. Add material.** See finding 1. The demo cannot record or take photos (no microphone, no photo folders), so only a text file was added. After adding, the subject page lists it by name as "Your note" with "This step is done. Next: The facts". That feedback is good.

**3. The subject page.** The left list of steps (Gather, Research, Simmer, Create, Share) with a state under each, and one "Next:" button, is easy to follow without reading. The raw header (finding 3) is the one thing that breaks it. "Say it again" carries a microphone icon although there is no speech. The standing chip, "Not decided yet, Gather first", did not change after the dish was named and a note added. Would slow him.

**4. Research.** The list, topic page and Find box read well. The topic page is clean: findings as sentences, one box for his line. "Listen" is shown though not built. The New topic form offers "Choose from the Library" and has no way to pick the subject's own material. Starting a topic, grouping and writing the one-line take are human-only and could not be tried.

**5. Create.** "Add a form" is a clear picker that says what each form needs. The Carousel adds in one press with a confirming line. See findings 2 and 4. Write, Read and Check open in Advanced as expected; Check shows its own page with "Check saved draft" and no result for a piece with nothing to compare. Back through "Version two" kept subject, form and step every time. In Advanced the Write side panel still labels the note with an internal phrase ("current-parent-material-source-words-not-editorial-instruction..."), and "Recover writing (1)" already shows on an untouched piece.

**6. Slide studio and Looks.** The studio on the demo's ready plan is strong: slide strip, large slide, layout choices, photographs and words tabs. Layout codes ("S7, C1, S1, P1", "C1 Night bleed") are shorthand he has to learn. Looks is easy to read: colours, type and logo with their values. Both look right in light and dark.

**7. Share and Outputs.** Version two has the step "Your yes" (two unticked statements and "Open the desk in Advanced") and "Post: Not known". Outputs has no version two screen. Not further testable.

**8. Leave and come back.** Reload keeps the subject, the form and the step. Opening another subject from the top chip and pressing Back returns to the first at the same step. The subject he made does not appear in the five desk cards on Home; it is in Everything. On a phone the bottom bar has Home, Research, Capture and Needs you only: Looks and Library are not reachable there.

**Reading and spacing.** Groups are set apart by surface and space on Home, the subject page, Research and Looks. The places that fail are the raw header (finding 3), the Article's words box on its step (a narrow nested box inside a box, with a repeated heading), and the bare studio page. No screen describes itself back to him except the repeated topic lines (finding 5).

## What I could not test, and why

- Anything human-only, because pages were opened as the agent: starting a topic, creating a group, adding a one-line take, the "Your yes" answers, and writing in Write. (Naming the dish was allowed to the agent and worked.)
- Recordings, photos and the photo picker: the demo has no microphone and no photo folders.
- Speech, "Make a layout", "Mix the layouts" and anything needing an AI provider: not connected.
- The Article through to Share, because an Article cannot start without words typed in Write.
- Publishing, sending and exporting.
- The installed app and real subjects, by rule.
- Light theme was checked once per screen listed at the top, not on every step.
