Tea tones for dark mode

Sencha is what the parts page uses now. Beside it, four more tea tones: the same colours every time (green, amber, red, blue, the warm accent), each with its own character.

Sencha

Gyokuro

Pu-erh

Sumi and matcha

Ink black, nearly neutral. Matcha green leads the signals

Silver needle

White tea. Lighter silvery greys, pale champagne signals
