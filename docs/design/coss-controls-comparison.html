With coss ui

What changes, in short

  • Buttons: softer corners, a fine highlight on top, a soft shadow; the main button glows slightly, the quiet ones get a faint outline.
  • Search box and dropdowns: a faint fill with a fine top edge, a soft ring when focused.
  • Station filters: one soft track with the chosen one raised, counts in small badges.
  • Rows and covers: a joined pair of buttons instead of two words.
  • Icon buttons: a small label appears on hover (Move up, Pin to your desk); the three dots open a menu.
  • Type stays Plus Jakarta Sans. coss's own font, Inter, can be compared on the parts page with the Font switch.
