# Wisdom bank: the concept

11 November 2026. Written after a short interview with Adrian, from [the synthesis](03-synthesis.md) and [the inquiry plan](../../plans/2026-11-11-09-wisdom-bank-inquiry.md). Nothing here is designed or built. The next thread makes three mock-ups from this page.

## The whole idea in one minute

The wisdom bank is the Research station of AR Create. It keeps the best lines from the talks and books you love, in their exact words, and helps you use them. Follow one example from start to finish:

1. **You paste a talk** on Vajrayana. You are asked nothing else.
2. **A few minutes later you are handed its best lines.** Each one is a **nugget**: the exact words, who said them, and the moment they said it. Under it sits one line saying what it means, and a plain explanation through your own filter. You keep the ones you love.
3. **The bank proposes connections.** "This line echoes one from your friend." "This practice comes before that idea." You say yes or no.
4. **One day you feel something you want to express**, such as "everything is perfect". You say it to the bank. It gathers what the different traditions said about it, in their own words, and what each says must come first.
5. **You pick a few and put them in order.** That line of nuggets is a research topic.
6. **You press "make a piece".** The topic becomes the outline of an article, a reel or the words for an artwork, with every quote credited.

Three promises hold throughout: their words are never changed or mixed with yours, nothing is linked or written without your yes, and you never have a pile to sort.

Everything below is the detail behind those six steps. Open a heading only when you want it.

## What the answers change

- **You return in order to use**: to express something, to study, or to browse until something rings. So the main door is "I have something to express", and a daily habit is only a small extra.
- **Assimilating means a lens.** The words stay untouched. Beside them sits an explanation written through your own set of variables, which you can change. This page calls that set your **lens**.
- **The pathway is the map.** A connects with B connects with C. A saved line of nuggets is what you walk, and it is also the outline of the piece you make.

## The nugget

One idea from one source. It holds:

- **The exact words**, checked letter for letter against the stored source. They can never be edited.
- **Who said them and where**: the speaker, the source, and the moment (minute and second in a talk, chapter and page in a book, date of a conversation).
- **A distillation**: one line, labelled "Distillation", written by an agent. You may reword it.
- **Its prime concepts**: the two or three ideas inside it, such as presence or preparation. Links attach through these.
- **A reading through your lens**: a short explanation labelled with the lens it used. Change the lens and the reading is written again. The words do not move.
- **Your response**: your own words, dated, in their own place.
- **Its links** to other nuggets, each with a kind and a reason.
- **Its brightness**: a nugget grows brighter as you keep it, respond to it, link it and use it. Dim ones stay. Nothing is killed.

**At a glance** you see the words, the speaker, the one line, and one or two lines running out to named neighbours: this goes into that. **Opened**, it unpacks as a short stack of index cards you swipe through: the words in their place in the source (with a tap to hear or read the moment); the gist and prime concepts; the reading through your lens; its connections; your response; and "go deeper", where questions wait for research.

## Arriving

You paste a link or drop a file, from the phone's share button or from anywhere in AR Create. That is the whole step. You are asked nothing. You may add one spoken line on why it struck you, or skip it.

The source is stored once. Then the work depends on its weight:

- **A video or an article** is distilled in a few minutes into a short set of proposed nuggets.
- **A book** is heavy. You choose: the whole book, one chapter at a time, or only the passages you marked. You see the likely cost before a heavy run starts.
- **A friend's idea** arrives as a recording or a note. The friend is the speaker. It stays marked private until they agree to be quoted. If it is your memory of what they said, it is labelled "as Adrian remembers it" and is never shown as a quote.

What comes back is a **source page**: the shape of the whole argument as an outline, with the proposed nuggets sitting in order inside it. The agent marks the ones it thinks are core. For each you **keep**, **reword the distillation**, or **set aside**. Set-aside nuggets are filed, and you can bring them back. Names, numbers and dates are flagged for a second look. Sorting and linking come later. A source you have not reviewed waits quietly.

## Returning

Five doors, in the order your answers put them.

1. **I have something to express.** You say what you are feeling or want to explain, for example "everything is perfect". The bank answers with the nuggets that say it, grouped by tradition, in their exact words, and shows what each tradition places before it. You choose some and they become a pathway. This is the main door.
2. **Wander.** Open any nugget and step to a neighbour, then the next. Brighter ones come first. This is for browsing until something rings true.
3. **Study a source.** Continue where you were. The source reads as a comfortable page with its outline, its nuggets in place and the lens readings beside them.
4. **Ask the bank.** A conversation that answers only from what you have kept. Every sentence shows its nugget. When the bank has nothing, it says so and offers to send the question to Research.
5. **A few today.** A small strip on the home screen: one bright nugget, one you have not seen for a while, one proposed link waiting for your yes.

The three traps, answered directly:

- **Collecting without returning.** Every door ends in something made: a pathway, a response, an accepted link or the start of a piece. Capture also gives something back at once, the source page, so putting things in is already a first return.
- **Maps nobody uses.** The home is never a picture of everything. The map is always centred on one nugget or one pathway, every line can be tapped, and every line says why it exists. A view of the whole bank may come later, for feeling it grow, and it stays only if you use it.
- **Summaries that flatten.** The exact words always come first and largest. A distillation says it is one. A lens reading names its lens. Your response is yours. The outline keeps each nugget attached to its argument, and the "prepares" link below keeps a concept attached to what must come before it.

## Connecting

When a source arrives, and whenever you ask, an agent proposes links. Each proposal shows the two nuggets side by side with one sentence of reason. You accept, change the kind, or decline. You can also draw a link yourself.

Five kinds, kept few on purpose:

- **Echoes**: another voice or tradition saying the same thing.
- **Prepares**: this must come before that. It is how the bank remembers that a concept without its preparation may be irresponsible.
- **Contrasts**: disagrees with it or sets a limit on it.
- **Explains**: unpacks it or gives an example.
- **Mine**: your own link, with your own words on it.

A **pathway** is a named line of nuggets in an order you chose, with your words between them. In version two it is the same thing as a research topic. The map is for four things: seeing what one idea goes into, walking from idea to idea, finding the missing step (a gap in a pathway becomes a research question, such as how Vajrayana arrives at "everything is perfect"), and feeling the whole thing join up over time.

## Leaving

From a pathway you choose "make a piece": an article, a script for a reel or video, or the words for an artwork. It opens as a Piece in AR Create with the pathway as its outline.

- Quotes arrive exact, with speaker and source. An agent may propose the prose around them and you decide, as everywhere in AR Create.
- Every sentence that draws on a nugget keeps a mark you can tap to see the words and the moment.
- A reel gets a short quote and a credit line for the screen. An artwork gets its words, a credit line and a longer note on sources.
- Before publishing, two gentle checks: a nugget from a private source asks whether the person has agreed, and a nugget with a "prepares" link you left out asks whether to include or acknowledge it.

## Where it lives

One home: the Research station of version two of AR Create. No third system.

- **Library** still stores every source once. The wisdom bank is the way Library is read: ideas first, files second.
- **The evidence card** AR Create already has (exact quote, position, source) grows into the nugget by gaining a speaker, a distillation, concepts, a lens reading, a response and links.
- **Collections** are questions with evidence and growing understanding. A pathway with a question is the same thing, so Collections become pathways and you learn one idea, not two.
- **Research** is "go deeper": a question from a nugget or a gap in a pathway. What it finds comes back as sources and proposed nuggets, still marked Supported or Uncertain.
- **The i64os shelf** stops being a second reading place. Its notes become sources in the bank, and nuggets are cut from them. Details below.
- **Your many entrances** (a pasted link, the phone's share button, an agent, the shelf, a note from the vault) all arrive at the same door as sources. The vault stays read-only.

**Version two: the bank lives in Research.** Also on 11 November you approved version two of the app, with four stations: Gather, Research, Create, Share. The existing workspace stays as Advanced. This concept was written against the existing names (Library, Collections, Research), and nothing in it depends on them. Asked where the bank lives, you answered on 11 November: "Yes, the bank lives inside the Research station." So the fit is:

- **Gather**: a talk, article or book comes in.
- **Research**: the bank itself. Nuggets, links, pathways, the map and the five doors.
- **Create**: "make a piece" from a pathway, with sources shown.
- **Share**: the credit lines and the checks before publishing.

Asked whether a pathway and a research topic are two things, you answered: "A pathway is the same thing as a research topic." So there is one idea to learn. A research topic in version two holds its groups of sources, as it does today, and gains the nuggets drawn from them, placed in an order you choose with your words between. Wherever this page says "pathway", the screen can say the topic's name.

The coordinating thread reports that version two's Research has topics and groups of sources so far, and no unit for one idea. If that is right, the nugget is what it lacks. I read the top of the version two plan and did not read its Research screens.

**What the i64os read found.** The separate thread reported on 11 November, after this concept was first written. It worked from i64os's documents and code and did not open the real data. Its page is `00b-i64os-inventory.md` on the review branch.

- **The shelf is real, and a note is a whole talk.** The documents say 189 notes, each at three depths, with no links between notes. So a shelf note is a source here, never a nugget.
- **Its summaries are retellings on purpose.** They can serve as the outline on a source page, labelled as a summary. They can never supply a nugget's words, so the bank needs its own distilling step that copies words exactly.
- **The exact words are already stored.** Every transcript is kept as timed lines, so a nugget's quote and moment can be cut from what you already have.
- **The Research Hunt has the right shape for "echoes" and "contrasts"**: one claim, and for each source an exact quote, its place and whether it agrees. It holds no real contents yet. Book search is built and, as far as the documents say, empty.
- **Collecting without returning is already happening.** 337 captured items have sat unsorted since 1 August. They should come in as sources that wait quietly and are distilled only when you open one or a question finds one, never as a queue to clear.

## Open questions only you can answer

1. **Your lens.** Which three to five variables make up your filter? The whole "assimilate" idea rests on these.
2. **Books.** How do they reach the bank: an ebook file, your Kindle highlights, photographs of pages, an audiobook?
3. **Friends.** Will their ideas arrive as recordings or from your memory, and do you want to ask each friend once or each time before quoting them in public?
4. **A few today.** Do you want it at all, or only the doors you open on purpose?
5. **The whole map.** Do you want one view of everything at once, knowing it is for feeling the bank grow more than for finding things?
6. **One bank or several.** Is this only for understanding consciousness, or does tea and everything else live in the same bank?
7. **Its name.** Is "wisdom bank" what the screen should say?
