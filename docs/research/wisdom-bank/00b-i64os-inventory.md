## The four answers

**1. Does anything already hold one idea with its exact words and its place in the source?** Two things come close, and neither is a nugget. The claim ledger's vote holds an exact quote, a locator and a link back to the source, with a stance, so it is a quote in a place. The Hunt find holds one exact passage, checked to be a contiguous copy. Both belong to the Research Hunt, which holds no real rows. The shelf note holds the words and the place only separately: the transcript keeps every timed line, while the summary and analysis are the generator's paraphrase, with a start time at each section head. So the closest thing in real use is the timed transcript, which is the whole source, not one idea.

**2. Does anything link one note or claim to another across sources?** Yes, in the Hunt only. One claim collects a vote from each source that supports or contests it, and a source can record that it cites or came from another. A separate store of proposed relationships between items exists, with approve and reject. The shelf notes themselves are not linked to each other. A knowledge note gets a list of related notes by shared tags at capture, and the same video is kept to one identity by its normalized address.

**3. Reuse, merge, or missing.** Reuse: the transcript with timed lines, the source identity rule, the capture path from link to note, verdicts written into the note's own header, stacks, and the Hunt's exact quote, locator and stance vote. Merge with AR Create's version: the viewpoint step, since AR Create already plans to read the Hunt through its tools, and anything AR Create holds for exact words and protected quotes. Missing: a unit for one idea, with its exact words, speaker, and moment, kept apart from the summary; links between ideas across sources, outside the Hunt; and distilling that keeps words verbatim, since the summarizer's own prompt tells the model to retell and states claims in its own words. Books have chunks but no speaker or idea.

**4. Screenshots and prototypes.** The shelf's own visual proof is the test file for the three depths (list, analysis, transcript). It writes pictures to a folder that is not saved in the repository, so none exist to show. Closest committed picture: the research prototype of a subject page with Brief, Standard and Full depth, marked mock data, copied here as docs/research/wisdom-bank-i64os-research-prototype.png. Other pictures in the repository are WordForge pages, not the shelf. Rendering the real shelf would need the real store, so I did not.

## Details

**Shelf.** Six verdicts: Integrate, Stack, Write, Build, File, Trash; non-exclusive. Two older sets also exist: the analysis's own marks (revive, take, works, skip, unknown) and a set of six pile verdicts in the Hunt. Stack and Build ask for a destination; the open check is a real Stack or Build press from the browser, never joined end to end.

**Distilling.** One model call with a written prompt: a one-sentence central claim, three to five takeaways, three to six topic sections each with a copied timestamp, up to four "worth watching" moments, up to six possibilities. It says to state only what the transcript says, plain sentences, third person. A second pass checks possibilities against Adrian's own evidence and may add a ready prompt. Notes already hand-written are marked external and never overwritten. Length is about 22 words per minute.

**Library.** Chunks are a unit of retrieval, not of meaning. Beliefs are not drawn from books.

**Hunt.** Operator-triggered only. Angles persist on the pile and count as coverage. Keep and reject stay with the operator. Agents reach it through seven tools; AR Create's research step is rewritten to call them.

**Planned work touching this.** The one-writing-system plan makes AR Create the writing room and i64os the engine for research, library and transcription. The How I Work plan puts "his exact words (protected)" as a block kind on AR Create's board, so the exact-words unit is being planned on the AR Create side. Neither plan builds a cross-source idea store.
