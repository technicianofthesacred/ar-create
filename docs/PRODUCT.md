# AR Create — what we are building

Living product requirements · maintained 14 October 2026.

This is the entry point for product direction. It connects the purpose, non-negotiable behavior, twelve section briefs and Delivery's work checklist. Requirements describe the intended product; they do not prove that it works today.

## The purpose

Help the author turn lived experiences, conversations, recordings, notes and media into work they are proud to publish or use. AI helps with preparation, research, organization, alternatives and production. The author directs meaning, preserves their own words and provides the final editorial polish.

One workspace should make that collaboration visible and enjoyable. Open a project, see where it stands, pick up the relevant work, make a meaningful contribution and return later without losing decisions or edits. The interface expresses the same underlying work that agents and existing file-based tools use. It is neither a second Magazine workflow nor a replacement for the author's judgment.

Projects can begin with a recording, supplied transcript, typed idea, photo, link or collection. Material remains useful even when it never becomes an article. A project can produce independently edited articles, carousels, scripts and other requested expressions from shared origins. No recording-first, article-first or every-format requirement is implied.

## Priorities made explicit from the author's feedback

- **Author-led production:** AI should do most preparation and structuring so the author can concentrate on editorial judgment, lived experience and final polish. Do not require manual quote extraction, repeated answers or assembly chores merely to make agent work visible. Deliver useful content before expanding the workspace into an unrelated project/commerce system.
- **Reuse the work already developed:** inspect and preserve the original Magazine commands, prompts, ordered research/brief/direction/drafting process and editorial iteration. Reuse AR's existing editing/recovery and approved portrait Instagram templates. Name what is being reused and the concrete gap before proposing a replacement. Do not invent another voice/template/process engine.
- **Understand before proposing:** incoming material is processed and understood before AI presents informed story directions, titles or a new draft. Text, photos and imported transcripts are valid starts. Existing projects can resume later work without repeating intake or approval; source readiness is not a compulsory recording-first path.
- **Show work wherever it happened:** agent/command work performed outside the interface must appear as readable research, decisions, words and media through the existing file connections. Do not ask for repeated import/answers or mark a phase incomplete just because no new action happened in the UI. Retained historical analysis and current reviewed work remain distinguishable.
- **Make the page potent, not fragmented:** reading and editing should feel like an enhanced text document. Keep a whole passage or carousel sequence available, with relevant content scrolling naturally; avoid repeated expansion clicks, giant inline alternatives and excessive headings. Paragraphs, sections and media can move without losing associated decisions or invalidating context silently.
- **Make guidance actionable:** voice, perspective, recipe, sources and versions should show their purpose, current choice, applicable scope and effect on the actual piece. Offer editable examples/contextual suggestions and recovery, not unexplained buttons or repeated setup forms. The author can open and edit the existing human-readable voice/expression sheet that AI uses, with one shared source rather than a separate profile. Reuse existing guidance and distinguish a pending suggestion from a change already applied.
- **Reward useful effort through clarity:** the project browser shows one concise project title and its current step, supports new projects and filing, and follows the selected light/dark theme. Resume the author's own work; don't substitute a generic file tree. Show how a contribution changed the current work and what meaningful action is next, without invented progress or gamification.
- **Prove the direction visually:** before a new major section direction is implemented, offer at least two focused options for the unresolved design choice. Existing chosen aspects remain respected; do not repeatedly reopen them. The current document-first foundation is an evolving direction, not blanket acceptance of every surface. Use targeted Impeccable critique when useful; verify actual screen interactions as well as file behavior.

### Intended private access

Software has its own AR Create repository; i64 OS is its host/entrance, and Teajia supplies relevant content and research. Private text and revision/decision records are intended to be available online on the author's private server and as computer replicas for offline work. Large originals may remain on the chosen always-on desktop or laptop, with explicit availability and privately accessible previews. Phone access must be authenticated and must explain which originals are available. This topology is an agreed product direction, not installed synchronization or authorization to transmit content. Preserve offline edits and conflicting versions; prove backup/restore independently of synchronization.

## Product pillars and shared acceptance

These requirements apply across section boundaries. Each scoped delivery names the relevant IDs and proves the affected behavior; none is complete merely because a button or record exists.

| ID | Pillar | Observable acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C01 | Human creative control | Reflections retain their attribution; protected phrases remain exact; corrections retain the original and identify affected work; chosen direction guides the intended output; rejection reasons prevent repeated unwanted suggestions; direct edits become current text; changes of mind visibly supersede prior decisions. Preserve project, part, passage and output scope. |
| C02 | Easy project pickup | Browse all projects, see useful progress, create/file a project, switch parts and outputs, then return to the right work and position without losing changes. |
| C03 | Shared material, independent expressions | One source collection supports separate output wording, layout, review and delivery states. A change flags only affected work for review; it never silently replaces human edits or makes all sibling outputs stale merely because they share origins. |
| C04 | Trustworthy files and recovery | Finder, the interface and agents refer to the same underlying material with stable identities and revisions. Preserve originals, unsaved work, saved Undo, conflict handling and historical editions. Show unavailable files honestly. |
| C05 | Joy through clarity | A compact sans-serif workspace, coherent themes, persistent project navigation and continuous document editing. Useful controls sit beside the relevant work. Contextual margin is visible by default with optional Focus. Whole carousels are editable together. Inspect actual wide/narrow and light/dark screens. |
| C06 | Honest AI and evidence | Distinguish transcript speech, personal reflection, outside evidence and AI proposals. Show processing, failures, uncertainty and unsupported actions. Saved analysis, mechanical checks, synthetic tests and live model execution are different facts. Suggestions need explicit human acceptance. |
| C07 | Private content and portable access | Keep software in Git and personal material/configuration outside it. Private text replicas, offline work, large-media availability and phone access require their own implementation and recovery proof; they are not established by a source push or local launcher. |

### The author's decisions carry forward

The visible current decision accompanies relevant research, direction, draft, arrangement and output review. Do not make the person repeat their opening, correction or rejection at each stage. Local decisions stay local unless broader intent is expressed. Preserve prior decisions with the reason for supersession. If exact downstream impact cannot be established, explain that review is needed rather than inventing a precise dependency.

Browsing an earlier checkpoint is not a stage reset, approval or permission to overwrite later work. Show retained earlier material separately from the current editable version and give a clear route back.

## Twelve sections: the work each must support

The linked brief is the detailed authority for that section. The checklist below names its required result, not current completion. Keep the twelve underlying checkpoints while sharing continuous work surfaces where useful. Materials and media remain accessible throughout; stages are not ceremonial gates.

| ID / section brief | Required work and human contribution | Result that carries forward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S01 · [Material](sections/01-material.md) | Bring more files, recordings, text, links and notes in; inspect what deserves keeping; preview, organize and connect whole conversations/resources; choose opening, closing and supporting context; start applicable processing. | Original-linked collection, meaningful groups, processing state and scoped personal context; explicit selected material for the next work. |
| S02 · [Transcripts & sources](sections/02-transcripts.md) | Transcribe or use supplied transcripts; read several sessions; compare/correct terms and speakers; preserve uncertainty and translations; play associated audio where actually connected. | Versioned corrected sources, attribution and explicit audio/transcript relationships; corrections flag affected later work. |
| S03 · [Research](sections/03-research.md) | Learn from unique conversation points; consult evidence and connected Tea Atlas; distinguish fact, opinion and wisdom; discuss a point, qualify claims, Include/Leave out, and develop a reusable brief. | Evidence-linked understanding and human choices, with honest verification/model status; knowledge enriches rather than replaces personal experience. |
| S04 · [Direction](sections/04-direction.md) | Compare story/title/structure options; write an original angle; choose audience, language and desired outputs; reject alternatives with reasons. | A scoped plan for each requested piece and retained alternatives; choosing a direction is not prose/publication approval. |
| S05 · [Draft](sections/05-draft.md) | Resume or develop current words using the plan/research/context; review first-person opening samples before a full new draft; type directly; request selected-passage help in context; save/recover. | Durable independent output wording, accepted edits and pending proposals linked to sources and decisions. |
| S06 · [Read & refine](sections/06-read-refine.md) | Read and polish the same manuscript; comment beside a passage; compare/accept/reject alternatives; protect deliberate wording and make structural edits. | The author's current text and explicit requests/decisions, with affected checks and approval identified. |
| S07 · [Check changes](sections/07-check-changes.md) | Reconcile direct edits and AI requests; inspect anchored findings, quote fidelity and remaining editorial questions; jump to passages/sources; rerun only relevant checks. | Revision-bound findings and resolved/held issues; mechanical success remains distinct from evidence, visual and publication approval. |
| S08 · [Media](sections/08-media.md) | Inspect photos and videos together; add, preview/play, organize, select, caption and credit; see unused and used-where/order; consume existing source-linked footage selections. | The same material enriched with metadata and selections; usage comes from actual saved placements. Selection is not placement or final approval. |
| S09 · [Arrange](sections/09-arrange.md) | Place words/media in the selected output; edit the whole carousel sequence; reorder/swap; crop each occurrence nondestructively; use supported video ranges and linked script scenes. | Independent output composition with source references, ordering, captions/credits and transforms; changes appear in Media's used-where view. |
| S10 · [Review & release](sections/10-review-release.md) | See the actual audience-facing article/carousel/other supported output; resolve readiness issues; compare/edit linked translations; approve the exact edition; export or explicitly request publication. | Correct reviewed edition and export/publication receipts; preview, exported, submitted and verified live remain separate. |
| S11 · [Share & celebrate](sections/11-share-celebrate.md) | Polish an invitation to people involved; translate/copy/export when needed; record actual sending/replies; discuss corrections or objections linked to the delivered piece. | Private recipient correspondence and explicit correction decisions; no sending by implication and an honest not-applicable/waiting route. |
| S12 · [Published & complete](sections/12-published-complete.md) | See every requested output and its real next action; open actual content/exports/delivered editions; resolve remaining work, begin a later edition or file the project. | Per-output destinations, dates, edition history and justified completion; no disabled examples or bare Confirmed label counted as usable. |

## How requirements connect to the work checklist

There is one execution list: **Delivery's existing private work checklist**. Delivery's current-state pointer locates that list and its proof. Do not create another TODO list here or copy private interviews, screenshots, requests or operational histories into Git.

For each S/C requirement being worked on, that checklist records:

- What can actually be used now, and in which environment.
- The missing result, responsible owner and next action/dependency.
- Status: not started, building, ready to try, blocked or finished. Candidate proof is labeled separately from installed behavior.
- The brief/requirement IDs, exact result version and relevant evidence.
- Any remaining human choice, privacy permission or limitation.

A finished task does not make the whole section finished. Acceptance includes applicable empty, processing, failed, stale, read-only and unavailable states, not just a happy path. Compare each claimed result with the user's actual journey; preserve exact words, save/switch/reload/Undo and independent outputs wherever affected.

The existing integrator controls shared wiring and live activation. Delivery commissions useful background helpers and receives results; existing chats have no automatic shared context.

When direction changes:

1. Record the accepted intent in the affected brief and its dated decision note. Change this overview when purpose, pillars or a cross-section relationship changes.
2. Preserve the rejected/superseded intent and why it changed; distinguish proposals from adopted direction.
3. Update only affected checklist rows and dependencies, then send Delivery one actionable delta. Do not relay the full conversation through other chats.
4. Verify the actual result before marking it ready; show a working preview and specific feedback request in the working Build/Design/story chat.

This document guides authorized development. It does not authorize a new architecture, original writeback, provider activation, private-data upload, external publication or runtime cutover.

## Journey acceptance against the original intent

For a relevant release, walk a mixed-material project from its original sources and personal opening/closing context through understanding, brief/direction, direct edits and requested outputs. Also inspect a previously completed project through earlier retained phases, images and the actual delivered edition. Keep real private examples outside Git and use invented counterparts for repository tests. Missing historical records are shown honestly rather than manufactured.

Review the selected output as a whole, open every listed result, switch projects and return, then check save/reload/Undo and scoped decisions. Check whether controls are understandable without a manual and whether the work needs unnecessary repeated clicks. A functional connection alone does not satisfy visual or editorial acceptance.
