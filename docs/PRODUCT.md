# AR Create — what we are building

Living product requirements · maintained 18 October 2026.

This is the entry point for product direction. It connects the purpose, non-negotiable behavior, twelve section briefs and Delivery's work checklist. Requirements describe the intended product; they do not prove that it works today.

## The purpose

Help the author turn lived experiences, conversations, recordings, notes and media into work they are proud to publish or use. AI helps with preparation, research, organization, alternatives and production. The author directs meaning, preserves their own words and provides the final editorial polish.

One workspace should make that collaboration visible and enjoyable. Open a project, see where it stands, pick up the relevant work, make a meaningful contribution and return later without losing decisions or edits. The interface expresses the same underlying work that agents and existing file-based tools use. It is neither a second Magazine workflow nor a replacement for the author's judgment.

Projects can begin with a recording, supplied transcript, typed idea, photo, link or collection. Material remains useful even when it never becomes an article. A project can produce independently edited articles, carousels, scripts and other requested expressions from shared origins. No recording-first, article-first or every-format requirement is implied.

## Priorities made explicit from the author's feedback

- **Author-led production:** AI should do most preparation and structuring so the author can concentrate on editorial judgment, lived experience and final polish. Do not require manual quote extraction, repeated answers or assembly chores merely to make agent work visible. Deliver useful content before expanding the workspace into an unrelated project/commerce system.
- **Reuse the work already developed:** inspect and preserve the original Magazine commands, prompts, ordered research/brief/direction/drafting process and editorial iteration. Reuse AR's existing editing/recovery and approved portrait Instagram templates. Name what is being reused and the concrete gap before proposing a replacement. Do not invent another voice/template/process engine.
- **Understand before proposing:** incoming material is processed and understood before AI presents informed story directions, titles or a new draft. Text, photos and imported transcripts are valid starts. Existing projects can resume later work without repeating intake or approval; source readiness is not a compulsory recording-first path.
- **Show work wherever it happened:** agent/command work performed outside the interface must appear as readable research, decisions, words and media through the existing file connections. Do not ask for repeated import/answers or mark a phase incomplete just because no new action happened in the UI. Retained historical analysis and current reviewed work remain distinguishable.
- **Make the page potent, not fragmented:** reading and editing should feel like an enhanced text document. Keep a whole passage or carousel sequence available, with relevant content scrolling naturally; avoid repeated expansion clicks, giant inline alternatives and excessive headings. Paragraphs, sections and media can move without losing associated decisions or invalidating context silently.
- **Make guidance actionable:** voice, perspective, recipe, sources and versions should show their purpose, current choice, applicable scope and effect on the actual piece. Offer editable examples/contextual suggestions and recovery, not unexplained buttons or repeated setup forms. The author can open and edit the existing human-readable voice/expression sheet that AI uses, with one shared source rather than a separate profile. Reuse existing guidance and distinguish a pending suggestion from a change already applied.
- **Reward useful effort through clarity:** the project browser shows one concise project title and its current step, supports new projects and filing, and follows the selected light/dark theme. Resume the author's own work; don't substitute a generic file tree. Show how a contribution changed the current work and what meaningful action is next, without invented progress or gamification.
- **Prove the direction visually:** before implementing a consequential unresolved major-section direction, show focused alternatives for that choice. Alternatives are not a compulsory exercise for every section or each delivery. Existing chosen aspects remain respected; do not repeatedly reopen them. The current document-first foundation is an evolving direction, not blanket acceptance of every surface. Use targeted Impeccable critique when useful; verify actual screen interactions as well as file behavior.

## Accepted delivery priorities — 14 October 2026

### Guardrails from the critical review

- Incoming agent/file changes show origin, revision and what changed. Review conflicts with the current draft; never silently overwrite human work or present historical analysis as newly verified evidence.
- Writing remains dominant. Reveal move/structural controls when useful; keep highlights and margin assistance selective, with discussion attached to its actual context. More controls are not automatically a better work surface.
- Keep reusable voice guidance concise and distinguish general preferences from this piece's direction. Changes identify affected pending assistance; existing accepted output wording does not silently change or become uniform across every story.
- Summary cards speed ordinary sorting, but a summary alone is insufficient grounds for destructive deletion. Provide easy access to supporting dialogue and the full conversation; preserve the scope and explicit human decision for permanent removal.
- Bilingual turn labels follow the appropriate imported source convention and never infer real personal identity from language alone. Keep recording timing available for checking without interrupting the default read.
- Footage suggestions preserve useful scenes and expose uncertain movement for review. Story needs guide final selection; original recordings remain intact. Existing footage review does not grant automatic trimming of all clips.
- Design alternatives are for consequential unresolved choices. The earlier request for alternatives for every section does not require recurring design exercises after a direction is chosen. Respect accepted aspects and refine the working view with actual feedback.

Choosing between a document-like arrangement and other scratchboard arrangements belongs to the later visual design pass; no new editor engine, spatial canvas, database, duplicated writing authority or compulsory idea form is implied. Keep project search/resume, save/recovery/Undo, exact wording and scoped source protections.

**Future acceptance:** start with a blank idea and rough notes; connect an existing story, transcript/source and new writing; retain these references and the original notes through switching/reload; compare proposed titles/angles without accepting them automatically; create two independently editable pieces from selected idea material and revisit the scratchboard without losing notes or altering either piece. This is a requirement for later work, not a claim of installed concept-brainstorming capability.

## 18 October — Curated personal resource library, later design pass

**Purpose and scale:** AR Create needs an organized, substantial personal library across projects, not only files attached to the current story and not a small recent-items drawer. It holds resources the author has chosen to keep: their own writing/articles, conversations and transcripts, translations, saved reading, PDFs/ebooks and YouTube/video references or permitted clips. Curated describes the selection, not a low volume limit; the library can grow considerably. Projects are the places where work develops; the library is reusable source material. One resource can support several concepts, tea collections and outputs without duplication. These requirements are for a later design pass; the active Claude Design version retains its frozen scope.

**Organization is fundamental:** offer a full Library entrance with scalable search and browsing, clear filing/home locations, topic collections, resource-type/language filters and saved views where useful. Give the author clear access to their writing, conversations, reading/reference material and video. Exact labels/layout remain design choices. Translations stay linked to the original and are also findable by language/type; do not fragment related versions into unrelated duplicates. One primary resource identity can appear in multiple topic/project collections. Keep physical location and logical grouping distinguishable: organizing a collection does not silently move original files; file actions must respect existing owned/read-only folder connections. Large catalogs need bounded result rendering and useful previews, not every document or every text body loaded into a sidebar, screen or model context. Search must work with the author's remembered topic/meaning and available source metadata, rather than require exact filenames. No new database/index engine is chosen by this brief.

**Keep why it matters:** retain optional human reasons for keeping a resource, relevant themes, marked passages and links to work made from it. Avoid compulsory metadata forms; prepare organization suggestions and let the author correct them. A resource deliberately kept before entering a project does not repeat the unknown-intake keep/discard ceremony. New, unreviewed incoming material still uses the existing review process. Curation includes revisiting relevance and reversible filing/archive; do not infer bulk or permanent deletion authority.

**Personal relevance is not file format or factual certainty:** the author's own writing and conversations they participated in carry direct personal context. Their explicit passage marks, annotations and statements of relevance are stronger signals than merely owning an ebook. An unread/unmarked ebook remains a saved reference, not evidence of the author's views or complete reading. Do not assume all transcripts are the author's conversations; distinguish participated conversations from third-party talks/video transcripts. A highly relevant marked book passage can be more useful to the current concept than a broadly related personal conversation. Keep task relevance, personal connection and factual support separate; no rigid universal ranking or opaque importance score is required.

**Read coverage and passages:** distinguish saved, personally read/annotated where actually known, and processed by AI. Unknown coverage stays unknown. AI processing a whole book does not mean the author read or endorsed it. A marked passage retains exact wording, original author/speaker, source identity/revision, original/translation relationship and the best actual location available: page/section for documents, speaker/turn for transcripts, genuine time range for audio/video. The whole source remains available. Keep source text, the author's note about it and AI interpretation distinct.

**Calling material into a concept:** when the author recalls several conversations or a related piece of writing, find candidate sources in the permitted library, show the matching passages and explain why each fits. Disambiguate only when necessary and keep a route to full context. Bring selected references into the scratchboard/research/brief without moving originals, silently approving claims or requiring reimport. Surface explicitly marked/connected material and relevant personal context while allowing broader reference exploration. Preserve uncertainty about who said what and what the author has personally encountered. No automatic new provider connection or wholesale private-library transmission follows from retrieval intent.

**Reuse and honest present coverage:** existing source/collection services, transcript original/corrected/translation handling, project resource browsing and source-linked passage/evidence records are starting points. Code for those mechanisms does not prove a complete cross-project personal-library UI, meaning-based retrieval, all-format extraction/readers, reading-coverage tracking or personal-relevance behavior. Reuse existing stable identities/captures and editorial protections rather than creating a second source store.

**Future acceptance:** browse/search a substantial mixed-format curated catalog; find remembered contributions across multiple conversations, the author's writing and saved references; pin a precise passage in a partially read book without marking the entire book personally read; find a translation through its original; connect one resource to several projects without copies; retrieve exact passage/context into an idea and keep all links through source revision and project switching. Check empty/no-match/unavailable/unsupported-processing states and preserve originals and human decisions. This records intent, not installed functionality.
