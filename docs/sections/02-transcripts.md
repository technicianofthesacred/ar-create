# 2 — Transcripts & sources

Intent: understand and correct source material, including multiple transcripts/recordings within one project. A transcript can be imported directly or produced from a recording added in Material.

## Work surface

Continuous readable transcript with a compact session selector and an optional continuous all-sessions view. Preserve each session's identity and chronology; do not concatenate away attribution. Keep the corresponding audio controls and position available where real timestamps exist. Original, corrected and translated versions are distinguishable. Text-only source notes remain usable without a fake audio/transcription requirement.

## Human and AI work

AI transcribes/aligns/flags uncertain words and speaker turns through existing configured tools. Adrian corrects terms, resolves speakers, compares wording, supplies missing context and keeps uncertainties unresolved when necessary. A repeated-name correction previews selected occurrences across selected transcripts; it is not an uncontrolled global replacement. Retrying transcription creates a candidate version and cannot erase a corrected transcript.

## Saved result and handoff

Immutable raw source, versioned corrected source, speaker/segment links, original-language/translation relationship and correction decisions. Research knows the exact sources and unresolved passages it is using. A recording split over files or a transcript covering several recordings uses explicit mappings; no invented timestamps or provenance. Session order and original date versus inferred chronology stay distinct.

## Acceptance

Switch between synthetic sessions without losing edits or place; compare original/correction, save/reload and identify affected downstream work. Imported transcripts work even with no audio. Source correction is distinct from factual research and narrative rewriting.
