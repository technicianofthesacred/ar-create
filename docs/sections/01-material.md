# 1 — Material

Intent agreed 13 October: an active place to bring material in, explore what is available, organize it and bring selected resources together. This is more than a record of what was already dropped.

## First decision: does this belong here?

Owner clarification, 13 October: Material begins with incoming material, before it becomes a kept source or project attachment. Receiving a chat or recording does not imply that it deserves preservation in the working library, a story, Research, a product record or further processing. Passing remarks, irrelevant chats, interpreter tests and useless fragments may have no lasting value. The first review must explain whether they should be kept, removed from the working view or permanently deleted from the review library; a generic discard label is insufficient.

The assistant inspects incoming material and recommends Keep, Remove from working view, Permanently delete from review library, or Needs review, with a concrete lasting-value reason. Check story, knowledge, product information, supporting context and meaningful companion conversations before recommending permanent deletion. Adrian can review those recommendations together. Do not require an invented use or a metadata form. Reversible removal retains Restore; permanent review-library deletion has no ordinary Restore and clearly names its scope. Only retained material proceeds to cleaning, session organization and possible project use. Useful product information remains a reason to keep a conversation independently of story interest; it is not a reason to keep every buying exchange.

This is an intake decision in the existing Material workspace, not another mandatory stage or a new application. Discarding incoming/working material does not imply deleting existing independent backups or provider history. Any broader permanent deletion keeps its explicit scope. No new deletion operation has been implemented or performed by this clarification.

## Work surface

Proposed: compact project collection with useful list/thumbnail views, visible resource groups and an in-place preview. A clear Add action supports files/folders where permitted, recordings, supplied transcripts, photos/videos, pasted text/links and personal notes. Browse connected folders or existing library material without copying it into a second library. Voice capture, if supported and permitted on the device, creates an ordinary original recording with a receipt.

Each item exposes the next applicable action: play, transcribe, review transcript, inspect a document, read a captured link, annotate, group or assign to a part/output. Batch applicable work. Show queued/running/failed/ready and original location/availability. Never show an uploaded file as transcribed merely because it was received.

## Creative decisions and AI

Adrian groups sessions, combines selected resources, adds why they matter, assigns opening/closing/context roles and chooses what an output can use. AI can suggest groups, titles for provisional filing and processing actions; it cannot invent a final angle before understanding the sources. Linking one item to several groups/outputs preserves one original identity. Personal reflection stays attributed, separate from outside evidence.

## Saved result and handoff

Durable originals, attachment/relationship records, processing jobs and scoped human context. Section2 opens the exact recording/transcript chain; Research receives an explicit selected set, not every file in the folder. Late additions identify possibly affected work without silently rewriting it. Dragging into a group changes membership, not the physical original's location unless that move is explicit.

## Effort and folder behavior

The default view should explain what is present and what can happen next through the actual collection and actions. Provide friendly drag/drop from the computer and supported web inputs; show new material placed in an existing working folder through the same connections. Preserve folder/source identity rather than asking the author to copy items into a second library. A project browser communicates projects and progress; a file tree alone does not establish the purpose. AI prepares quote/source suggestions; manually extracting quotations is not an intake chore.

## Decision safety

Summary-based recommendations support fast sorting; they do not establish that every important passage has been inspected. Make the supporting passage and full conversation easy to reach. Permanent review-library deletion requires the explicit scoped human choice and enough evidence to review the decision; never perform it solely because a summary sounded unimportant. Reversible hiding/removal remains distinct.

## Acceptance

A synthetic multi-session project visibly groups multiple sessions with opening/closing context; add another resource, process it, organize it, select a subset and resume after switching stories. Interrupted imports, duplicates, unsupported types and offline originals have useful recovery. No mandatory reimport of existing material.

14 October clarification: strengthened interaction acceptance from direct human feedback; no installed-functionality claim.

## Distinct Material surface — 15 October clarification

Material is the collection and active entrance: show incoming/kept files, recordings, transcripts, notes, links and resource groups with Add, preview, organize, select and applicable processing actions. Its primary work is not the continuous corrected transcript displayed in Source. Selecting Review transcript opens that item's matching source/session in section 2. Unconnected tools remain visible with an honest preparation/not-connected state; an upload or click cannot stand in for a processing result.
