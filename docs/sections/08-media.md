# 8 — Media

Intent agreed 13 October: an interactive contact sheet of photos AND videos, with two-way visibility into where they are used. This is the visual view of the same assets collected in Material.

## Work surface

Show actual thumbnails/poster frames, quick preview/playback, useful grouping/filtering and multi-select. Distinguish candidate, selected, placed and unused; selecting is not placing. Show original availability and processing failures. Add from device/bound folders/library without another independent media store.

Every used asset shows output name, location and order: article section/caption, carousel slide, or video occurrence. An asset can be used several times and in multiple outputs. Click an occurrence to open that exact placement. Filters can reveal unused assets, missing originals or a particular output's selections. Usage comes from saved composition references, never a manually maintained duplicate flag.

## Human and AI work

Adrian chooses media, identifies people/objects, edits captions/credits/alt text and can swap a placed image for another. AI suggests scene matches, descriptions and a useful organization based on actual inspection. Do not silently discard the rest when AI makes a selection. Keep original Magazine expectation that every eligible photo is considered and unresolved important unpictured moments are visible.

Basic crop/rotation/focal-point adjustment is nondestructive. Asset defaults can help future placement, but output-specific crops belong to each occurrence in Arrange. Video playback, duration and poster selection are needed; output-specific trim/range support must be scoped before implementation, not expanded into a full video editor. Preserve originals and do not alter every use when adjusting one.

## Select for this piece

The story/output’s needs guide which proposed shots are selected. Preserving useful original ranges does not require presenting every range as chosen footage or retaining every proposed occurrence in the final output. Flag questionable movement and retain easy access to original context; trimming recommendations do not silently cut or delete source recordings.

## Result and acceptance

Media metadata, human selections and derivatives are linked to stable asset IDs. Arrange changes immediately update used-where/order here. A swap preserves placement/caption/credit intentionally and flags any mismatch for review; Undo restores both views. Reorder a carousel and see the new order here after save/reload. Photos/videos are available at any text stage; text-only outputs remain valid.

## Reuse the existing footage work

Use the existing footage-review process for screening, proposed shot boundaries, full-context review and reusable selections. AR Create should consume those saved original-linked selections, not run a competing trimming process. Preserve all useful source ranges rather than reducing everything to short highlights. Camera-movement style acceptance, individual selection acceptance and final output approval remain distinct.

Show video originals and proposed/approved shot ranges in the same project material/media collection. People can play a range, see the surrounding original, keep/check/reject a proposal, and select footage for an output. Preserve the source timebase and original in/out times, orientation/audio and explicit availability. A shot is a reference to a range, not a replacement for its original recording. Poster frames and playback previews remain linked derivatives.

Premiere remains available for the full footage review and detailed editing. The first connection may consume a dated selection snapshot; it must say so. Human edits made in Premiere after that snapshot require explicit reconciliation and must not be overwritten or described as automatic live sync. Private file locations, footage, thumbnails and real selection histories remain outside Git and model-provider transmission.

## Preserve useful movement when selecting footage

Carry the existing footage review's chosen calibration into the selection handoff. Keep whole usable actions and their relevant lead-in/out rather than cutting a long useful movement to a tiny central highlight. Consider meaningful motion or direction changes when proposing boundaries. Flag very short clips and visibly failed rapid, jumpy or choppy camera movement for review; where only part fails, retain useful footage before and after it. These are review criteria, not blanket automatic cutting or deletion permission. The footage owner retains its detailed calibration, full-context review and original ranges; AR Create reuses that evidence rather than rescreening or inventing a new trimming engine.
