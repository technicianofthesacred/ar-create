# 8 — Media

Intent agreed 13 October: an interactive contact sheet of photos AND videos, with two-way visibility into where they are used. This is the visual view of the same assets collected in Material.

## Work surface

Show actual thumbnails/poster frames, quick preview/playback, useful grouping/filtering and multi-select. Distinguish candidate, selected, placed and unused; selecting is not placing. Show original availability and processing failures. Add from device/bound folders/library without another independent media store.

Every used asset shows output name, location and order: article section/caption, carousel slide, or video occurrence. An asset can be used several times and in multiple outputs. Click an occurrence to open that exact placement. Filters can reveal unused assets, missing originals or a particular output's selections. Usage comes from saved composition references, never a manually maintained duplicate flag.

## Human and AI work

Adrian chooses media, identifies people/objects, edits captions/credits/alt text and can swap a placed image for another. AI suggests scene matches, descriptions and a useful organization based on actual inspection. Do not silently discard the rest when AI makes a selection. Keep original Magazine expectation that every eligible photo is considered and unresolved important unpictured moments are visible.

Basic crop/rotation/focal-point adjustment is nondestructive. Asset defaults can help future placement, but output-specific crops belong to each occurrence in Arrange. Video playback, duration and poster selection are needed; output-specific trim/range support must be scoped before implementation, not expanded into a full video editor. Preserve originals and do not alter every use when adjusting one.

## Result and acceptance

Media metadata, human selections and derivatives are linked to stable asset IDs. Arrange changes immediately update used-where/order here. A swap preserves placement/caption/credit intentionally and flags any mismatch for review; Undo restores both views. Reorder a carousel and see the new order here after save/reload. Photos/videos are available at any text stage; text-only outputs remain valid.
