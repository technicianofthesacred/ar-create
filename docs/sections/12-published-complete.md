## Completion and history

An output is finished when it reaches its intended destination, such as a published article or an approved script export for filming, with any agreed corrections or follow-up resolved. Completion is tracked independently per output and against its actual delivered edition. Paused outputs remain paused; unrequested formats create no obligations.

Published date, export date and completion date are different. Save receipts from actual events or clearly attributed human records; never infer publication from a saved file or use today's date to fill missing history. Preserve the original checkpoint12 meaning: applicable feedback/corrections resolved and delivered result/files reconciled. A new edit creates a later edition, preserving the old published one. Completing an article cannot mark its untranslated WeChat version or unfinished carousel complete. Unrequested formats do not become compulsory work.
