# 10 — Review & release

Intent: see and control what each audience receives, including translated and platform-specific expressions.

## Work surface

Actual clean rendering of the selected output: article, full carousel, print/physical article, WeChat article or Twitter/X post as supported. Compact output/language selector, concrete readiness issues and direct jumps to the place needing work. Separate authoring Arrange from this reader view without creating duplicate content.

Provide a dedicated Translation & export area within this workflow: create a linked language/platform variant, compare original and translation, edit translated wording, retain approved names/terms/quotes, preview target formatting and export/copy a package appropriate to the verified destination. Chinese-language adaptation may change introductions and contextual explanation, but those are reviewable editorial changes, not hidden literal translation. A translated follow-up message remains available in Share & celebrate too.

## Decisions and result

Adrian approves the exact language/output revision and separately requests actual publication. AI can translate, suggest audience adaptations and flag unsupported formatting; no silent provider activation. A source change marks affected variants for review. Translation/export preserve provenance and never imply WeChat/X API integration exists.

Save review decisions, export receipts/files and actual publish status per output. Exported, scheduled if genuinely supported, submitted, failed and verified live are distinct. Preview cannot silently use thumbnails where full-resolution originals are required. A physical article can be complete as a reviewed print-ready export without a public URL.

## Acceptance

Review article and carousel independently; update source text and see relevant translation/approval become stale; export correct files with required assets/credits. Test real platform capabilities before promising format fidelity or automated publication. All slides remain reviewable together.

## Separate a working draft from an approved output

A private download of the author’s current saved draft may retain unresolved review marks and editorial instructions. Label it as a draft with its actual format; verify the download against saved bytes and scope. Do not require silent acceptance of those passages merely to recover or carry the working text elsewhere. Keep designed-output review and publication protections intact. A draft Markdown download is not proof that article, carousel, translation or platform exports are approved or finished.
