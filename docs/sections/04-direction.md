# 4 — Direction

Intent: choose what Adrian wants to express after understanding the material, without discarding other possible stories.

## Work surface

A readable map with a few consequential story directions, title options and short plans; reuse original Magazine requirements. Show differences in emphasis, structure and audience, with chronological direction where appropriate. Let Adrian write his own angle. Keep source selection secondary and resulting plan immediately visible.

## Decisions and AI

Adrian selects/edits direction, title, intended audience/language and requested output(s), contributes opening/closing intent and rejects unwanted angles with reasons. AI explains fit from actual research and context, then prepares per-piece plans. A shared project direction and an output-specific direction are distinct.

## Result and acceptance

Dated scoped decisions, retained alternatives and a plan per requested piece/output. Continue opens the matching manuscript part. Later changes visibly supersede earlier decisions and identify affected drafts/translations without automatic regeneration. No extra source import or repeated answers across stages. A choice is not approval of prose or publication.
