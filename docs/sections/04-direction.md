## Decisions and AI

AI explains fit from actual research and context, then prepares per-piece plans. A shared project direction and an output-specific direction are distinct. Decisions default to the output or passage currently being discussed unless broader intent is expressed. Clearly broader instructions retain their intended project or multi-output scope. Ask for scope clarification only when ambiguity materially affects the work; do not interrupt ordinary edits with routine scope questions.

## Result and acceptance

Dated scoped decisions, retained alternatives and a plan per requested piece/output. Continue opens the matching manuscript part. Later changes visibly supersede earlier decisions and identify affected drafts/translations without automatic regeneration. No extra source import or repeated answers across stages. A choice is not approval of prose or publication.
