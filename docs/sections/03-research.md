## 16 October — Research is independently useful learning

The author wants Research to be a place to become educated and understand a topic much more deeply, rather than only preparation for writing. Content creation remains the main product context, but Research must be useful without selecting or creating an output. Preserve direct access and return-to-learning context, substantial explanations, source/evidence context, discussion, follow-up questions and the ability to add and deepen inquiry. Choosing what enters an article/carousel is a separate optional editorial action; output needs must not reduce Research to extracted facts, a brief or summary cards.

Standalone potential is a future direction, not authorization for a separate product, database, backend or provider. This clarification updates accepted intent only; no new implementation is requested here. Proposed grouped navigation/unified writing modes remain unaccepted proposals. Existing scope/revision/source and human-decision protections apply to learning records as well as editorial reuse. Current output-dependent interface contracts require separately scoped reconciliation before independent-learning completion can be claimed.

## Work surface and authority

Use the human-approved Research reference, exact readable layout/interactions adapted to app palette. Reference owner maintains the frozen files and exact-match comparison; a temporary port is not source authority. Findings lead the page; operational source selection stays in a compact disclosure. Each meaningful point connects what was said, speaker/time where known, evidence and uncertainty, editorial implication and optional in-context narration proposal. No invented quote to fill a template.

## Learning comes first

Lead with the unique point the author wants to understand, the actual source quotation and speaker/time when known, then a clear explanation and useful further inquiry. Preserve the chosen readable spacing, Supported/Uncertain distinction, suggested shifts shown in actual story context, disclosed sources and per-point AI discussion. Claim checking supports this learning experience; it is not a replacement report divided into opaque claims/wisdom/speaker boxes. Reuse actual Magazine claim/evidence/brief logic and connected Tea Atlas entries; identify missing connections and external evidence honestly. Source understanding produces a reusable brief before informed story directions or drafting.

Existing agent-produced research must remain readable here, with its original revision/status and unresolved questions. Absence of a new UI run does not erase earlier research; retained analysis cannot be silently upgraded to current verification.

AI distinguishes fact/opinion/wisdom, checks evidence quality, traces teachings and proposes further inquiry. Retained notes are not external verification. Actual model availability is explicit.

## Recover the full existing research process — 15 October clarification

The author rejects a shallow summaries-only Research surface. Reuse the original Magazine and shared research/brief contracts, and the maintained Wordforge extraction/import/inheritance primitives where applicable. Keep the approved readable per-point learning presentation; the depth below is content and capability, not a request to return to opaque report boxes.

- **Understand and expand:** exact source words, speaker and genuine time/scene; explain distinctive ideas, history, terms, misconceptions and turning points. Trace teachings to primary texts/traditions as connections, never invent the speaker's influence. Retain worthwhile quieter human lessons and untraced questions.
- **Evaluate evidence:** distinguish factual claims, attributed opinion and wisdom. Facts have usable, qualified with exact limiting wording, or held status; disclose supporting/conflicting sources and their quality. A source excerpt or mechanical match alone is not verification.
- **Protect the people and the author's meaning:** show each flagged speaker/editorial issue with original wording, proposed handling and reason. Offer the existing Suggested/As said/Cut/My words decisions with context, narration correction or whole-phrase trimming as applicable. Never repair researched facts by silently rewriting a quotation. These judgments and Include/Leave out are different decisions, both owned by the human.
- **Learn and discuss:** per-point questions, follow-up, lived context, source selection and contextual narration proposals. Preserve discussion and proposed/applied distinctions. Show what the author can now share, what each other speaker learned, useful next questions/visits/reads, teach points/terms and threads across related recordings.
- **Prepare and maintain the reusable brief:** central line, moments, exact permitted quotes, usable/qualified additions and traced teachings, teach points, held items/reasons, related threads and outputs made from it. Open existing work and show dated updates when evidence or a reply changes what may be used. Do not replace the shared brief with a second UI-only summary.
- **Carry the result into Direction:** informed angle/title/structure choices consume the selected sources, brief and dated human decisions. Existing completed work resumes without repeated processing; new choices cannot silently rewrite current drafts or independent outputs.

Place these intended interactions on the current Research surface even where backend work remains. Show retained findings and genuine current evidence, and clearly label unconnected external research, Atlas retrieval, model discussion or brief generation. No illustrative quote/finding/verdict may appear as a real result. Do not run providers or publish/send anything merely to demonstrate the interface. The design handoff can proceed with honest unconnected tools; functional proof remains a separate acceptance.
