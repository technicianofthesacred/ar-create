# 11 — Share & celebrate

Intent agreed 13 October: a personal invitation and celebration sent to people involved, also asking whether the piece feels good and represents them accurately. This is more than an administrative feedback request.

## Work surface

An editable recipient-specific message with the finished piece/link, a warm invitation, relevant shared learning and a few contextual questions. Reuse Magazine's feedback template and What we learned. Include name/introduction/photo checks where unresolved. Show source-language and translated message when needed, with copy/export suitable for WeChat or another selected channel. Recipient details and correspondence stay private.

## Human and AI work

AI drafts or translates a message grounded in the actual relationship and piece; Adrian sets tone, polishes and sends or explicitly authorizes sending. Never pretend a message was sent because prepared/copied. Record actual sent date/channel, append replies, translate them, and distinguish appreciation from an actionable correction or objection. A reply proposing a factual/name/photo correction links to affected outputs for Adrian's decision; it cannot silently rewrite published content.

## Result and acceptance

Save message versions, language, target published edition, per-recipient sent/replied state, replies and proposed corrections. A message drafted before publication remains a draft; outdated links/editions are flagged. No recipient means an explicit not-applicable route. No reply means waiting or deliberately closed with reason, not confirmed. Completing one recipient's conversation does not finish every output.
