## Stage B: one header for every screen (needs decision 5)

Today each screen places its title at a different position and size, and its actions stop at a different right edge (1232, 1296, 1328 or 1408px at desktop).

- One header line: the step's name at the same left edge, height and size; the subject's name after it; the actions right-aligned to one shared edge, with one primary action at most.
- Content starts at the same left edge on every screen. Research is no longer a narrow centred column: left-aligned with a readable line length.
- Outputs: "By kind" and "More" join "New output" on the header line.
- Home: "Home" is the first thing on the page, with "Needs you" under it.
- Write, Read and Check keep the page geometry they have (16px text, about 700px wide); only the header line above them is aligned.
- Files: `canvas-c3.js`, `canvas-c3.css`, `surface-bar.js`, `section-refinement.css`, and each step's own stylesheet.

This moves titles on layouts Adrian has accepted, which is why it needs his yes before it starts.

## Stage C: menus and controls

- Menus (Piece tools, the "+" menu, More, the finder) become plain rows with a hover highlight and clear group names, in one width. Close and Back leave the "+" list. "More" has one "This piece" group. The finder shows each step's name once, without "Tool · current project".
- Home's subject rows: quiet rows with a separator and a hover highlight; the "..." inside the row; one edge on the filter field.
- Dropdowns styled like the buttons (Outputs, Media, Source, Direction).
- Media: no table headings, "Select all" or "Clear" over an empty list.
- Files: `combined-piece.css`, `combined-piece.js`, `home-tabs.js`, `home-tabs.css`, `project-library.js`, `graphite-controls.css`, `folder-browser.css`.

## Stage D: phone

- A one-row top bar: the subject's name, then Thought, Understanding, text size and theme as icons. This returns about 48px to the page.
- One header per screen: title, the one primary action, and "More". No unlabelled icon buttons.
- The bottom bar matches the desktop sidebar's groups (needs decision 6): add Gather (Library, Material, Source), so nothing is filed differently on the phone and something is always lit.
- Fields and dropdowns at least 44px high: "Filter projects", "Search sources", "Search media", the Source, Direction, Media and Outputs dropdowns, "Recover writing".
- Long lines wrap instead of being cut off.
- Files: `combined-phone.css`, `combined-navigation.js`.

## Stage E: text size (needs decision 8)

A− and A+ enlarge the writing and the side panel together and leave the top bar and sidebar fixed. Today part of each grows and part does not. Files: `c3-theme.css`, `draft-surface.css`.

## What must not change

- The Combined layout's regions, the Graphite controls, Plus Jakarta Sans for the interface and Lora for spoken words.
- Every action still presses its owner's real control; a disabled control stays disabled with its reason.
- No hard-coded colours or fonts; tokens only (`docs/product/LOOK.md`).
- No sideways scroll at any width (true today).
- Simmer, the Instagram studio and the output templates.
