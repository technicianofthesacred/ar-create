# Plan 4: plain words

**Goal.** One word for each thing, and every message says what happened and what to do next, in words Adrian would use.

**Findings covered.** U13, U14, U16, V13, V14, and the wording parts of U9, U10, U15, U17. The requirement is already written down in `docs/product/HUMAN-EXPERIENCE.md` ("Write for someone arriving without the history"); this plan applies it.

## Part A: one word per thing

Counted across all thirteen screens, Piece tools and Understanding: "output" 62 times and "piece" 13; "project" far more often than "subject"; "draft" 21, "edition" 7, "manuscript" 3, "working manuscript" 2.

Proposed list for Adrian to accept or change. Nothing is renamed until he has.

| The thing | Proposed word on screen | Words to retire from the screen |
|---|---|---|
| What he is working on (a tea, a place, a conversation) | **Subject** | project, study, collection (in this sense) |
| One thing he is making from it | **Piece** (an Article, a Carousel, a Post) | output, working copy, working manuscript |
| The words of a piece | **Writing** | manuscript, draft (as a noun), content, wording |
| A saved state of a piece he can go back to | **Version** | revision, edition, "r0" |
| What goes into a subject | **Material** (before it is attached), **Source** (once it is) | session, capture, original (except "the original recording") |
| What he wants a piece to say | **Direction** | angle and title stay as parts of it; brief, concept |
| What has been learned | **Understanding** | learning, notes (except "your notes") |
| The step names | Material, Source, Research, Direction, Write, Read, Check, Media, Arrange, Audience, Share, Outputs | unchanged |

"Outputs" stays as the name of the last step. Inside it, each row is a piece.

Dates use one form everywhere: "10 Nov 2026", and "10 Nov, 13:52" where the time matters.

## What must not change

- Honesty. "Not connected" tools still say they are not connected; nothing claims AI ran, a source was verified, work was saved or a piece was published without that being true.
- The difference between source speech, Adrian's reflection, outside evidence and AI proposals stays visible, and never by colour alone.
- Internal names stay as they are in code, routes, the CLI and files. This plan changes what is shown, not what things are called underneath (`docs/START.md` keeps the map of code names to product words).
- Exact text that the look modules search for. Where a label is changed, the module that matches on it is changed in the same commit (`docs/product/LOOK.md`, rule 5).

## Steps

1. Adrian accepts or edits the word list in Part A.
2. A test that reads the visible text of every screen in the demo and fails on a retired word outside Details. It starts as a report, and becomes a failing test screen by screen.
3. Apply Part A one step at a time (Home and the finder first, then Write and Outputs), with before and after screenshots.
4. Draft the Part B messages. Adrian reads them in the demo and approves or rewrites. The Check result goes first.
5. Update `docs/START.md`'s name table and `docs/product/HUMAN-EXPERIENCE.md` with the accepted list, so later work keeps it.

## Finish checks

- Someone who has never seen the app can say, after one read of the Check result, whether the piece passed and what to do next.
- No retired word is visible on any of the thirteen screens, Piece tools or Understanding, outside Details.
- Opening Direction's form writes nothing to the history.
- Home shows no failure message for an empty subject, and no script error.
- One date form on every screen.
- `npm test` passes, including the visible-words test.
