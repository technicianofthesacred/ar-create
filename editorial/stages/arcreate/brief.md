# Brief: one per recording

The brief is what everything else is made from, so the thinking is done once. It is a working file, a bare list, written right after research. Adrian does not need to read it; when he asks to, send it as a reading link.

Write it where the brand's profile says (Teajia: `MAG/Workflow/3-Develop/BRIEF - [Title].md`).

## Shape

```markdown
---
type: BRIEF
brand: Teajia
title: Hong Kong Day 1
recorded: 2026-10-03
speakers: Adrian; Mathew (Pu'er Medicine)
source: <the clean record's file>
research: <the research file>
---

## The line it stands on
One or two sentences, after research. ("Hong Kong storage" is a place, not a flavor.)

## Moments
- 05:20 Mathew stops mid-sentence as the tea hits
- …

## Quotes that can be used
Only lines research and the speaker check allow, each with speaker and time, word for word.
- Mathew, 05:20: "It was a wave."

## What research adds
The usable and qualified facts and the traced teachings worth teaching, one line each, with their state.
- usable: the 88 Qing was stored dry in Hong Kong from 1993
- qualified (Hong Kong drove demand for fermented tea; some orders were lighter): …

## Teach points
1. …

## Held
Anything not to use, and why, one line each (held back facts, flagged lines without a yes).

## Threads
Ideas shared with other recordings, with their titles.

## Made from this
Filled by make: date, what was made, where it went, which items above it used.
```

## Rules

- Quotes are copied from the clean record, never paraphrased.
- An item is either usable here or listed under Held. Nothing in between.
- Keep it to what a maker needs. The reasoning stays in the research file.
- A brief changes when new research or a speaker's reply changes what can be used; note the date beside the change.
