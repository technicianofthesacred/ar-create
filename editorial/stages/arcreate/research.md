# Research: check and deepen what was said

Adrian, 2026-10-04: "the goal is also to deeper educate people", and "before the whole story is written all these new things are considered." Research runs once the clean record exists and before anything is written from it. For a Teajia magazine story it is the first half of step 3, before the map.

Write the research file where the brand's profile says (Teajia: `MAG/Workflow/3-Develop/RESEARCH - [Title].md`), as a bare-list working file. Worked example of the thinking: the Hong Kong day 1 debrief, where checking turned "Hong Kong storage has a bad reputation" into "Hong Kong storage is a place, not a flavor" and surfaced the 88 Qing story.

A recording already past this point: run research once before the next thing made from it is shown to Adrian, and fix that thing from it.

## 1. Claims

Go through the clean record and list every fact, name, term, date and number, each with who said it and the time. Sort each into one kind:

- **Fact**: checkable. Search for it (web search, or firecrawl). Give it a verdict and its sources.
- **Opinion**: their taste or reading. Not checked; it stays as their quote.
- **Wisdom**: a teaching ("tea is about caring for the people you serve"). Not graded: expanded (section 3).

Verdict, and how anything made from it may use it (the same three states as the Tea Wisdom reference on teajia.com):

| Verdict | Use |
|---|---|
| confirmed | usable: may be stated as fact |
| corrected, or mixed | qualified: stated only with the limiting words, written down beside it |
| unconfirmed | held back: not stated as fact; may stay as the speaker's own view in their quote |

Note each source's quality, strongest first: scholarly work, a named person who was there, reputable press, reference works, then vendor and shop blogs and forums, which are leads only. A fact resting only on leads is qualified at best, and gets a "Read" line in section 5 pointing to a stronger source. Never present a shop used as a source as Adrian's supplier, and never carry one shop's claims about its product onto a different one.

A teaching that names its own source ("as Rikyu said") carries a fact: the attribution is checked like any other.

## 2. Expand

For each fact worth teaching, the history or story behind it that a reader would not know, with sources: a person, a batch, a turning point, the misconception it corrects, the term in its own language with characters and pinyin where it has them. One to three lines each. These are the material for narration, notes and teach points.

## 3. Wisdom

For each teaching, where the same idea also lives: a tradition, a text, a teacher, in its own wording where possible, and what that source adds or frames differently. Framed as a connection, never as the speaker's source ("the same idea runs through Rikyu's rules"), and sourced to primary texts, museums or scholars, not blogs. A teaching not yet traced stays in the list as "to trace", so it is not lost.

## 4. Speaker check

Every line that could make a speaker look wrong, careless or unkind in print:

- a fact research corrected, or a claim it could not support
- open doubt on the record ("or was that Korea")
- a line that reads as an attack on a person, shop, place or culture that can be recognised
- two terms run together so the speaker sounds less expert than they are ("Hong Kong storage" for wet storage)
- something personal they may not want printed
- a word the transcript was unsure of that would put a wrong name or term in their mouth

Rules:

- Never change words inside a quote. Making someone sound better by editing their quote is still misquoting.
- One action per line, and record it: **keep with context** (accurate, but a line around it keeps it fair), **correct in narration** (don't quote the slip), **trim the quote** to the part that holds (only cutting whole phrases, never rewording), **cut**, or **ask them to restate** it in their own words.
- The default is mine and never publishes the slip, but **Adrian decides every flagged line, his own and everyone else's**: he is the editor (Adrian, 2026-10-04: "I can decide for others as well, I'm the editor"). Never decide silently and leave him nothing to adjust ("where are the check marks for adjustment").
- **Every flagged line goes in the ledger.** For Teajia that is the story's ASK file on the magazine page, one question per line in the ledger shape (`who`, `at`, `said`, `suggested`, `why`, `telling: yes` when the suggestion is his narration, then Suggested (recommended) / As said / Cut / My words); the format is at the top of `~/builds/mag-ask.mjs`. The page shows them together as one ledger, set in Newsreader: the said words struck where they change, the chosen version's new words in bronze, so he decides on the words themselves. Next to it, one question on the line the story stands on, and one per fact only he can settle (which tea it was). Worked example: `ASK - Hong Kong Day 1.md`.
- A speaker never has to approve a line before it runs. If Adrian wants a guest to restate something in their own words, write a short note to them in their language, which he sends himself (Teajia: `6-Feedback/BEFORE - [Title] - [Name].md`), and nothing waits on it.
- A critical passage about a shop or person: never name or show them identifiably.

## 5. For Adrian, and for the people who spoke

- **What you can now share** (for Adrian): the corrected and new facts, each in one plain line, so he can say them in future talks. Then what to ask, taste or see next time, and one or two good reads. This is the only part of the research file he reads: send it as a reading link when this step stops.
- **What we learned** (for each other speaker): what research confirmed, corrected or added about what they said, in two or three short lines. It goes into the message they get after publishing, so they learn it too.
- **Teach points**: the one to three things a reader should leave knowing, and the terms worth defining. The brand's profile says where those go.
- **Threads**: an idea that also turns up in another recording's research. Name them. Three or more is a series worth raising with Adrian.
