# Research and brief: before the map

Research now lives in `/arcreate`, so the magazine and every other kind of content share one copy (Adrian, 2026-10-04). For a magazine story, at step 3 before the map:

1. Read and do [arcreate's research step](../../arcreate/stages/research.md), with [the Teajia profile](../../arcreate/brands/teajia.md) for where files go.
2. Then [arcreate's brief step](../../arcreate/stages/brief.md).
3. Then go on to [map.md](map.md). The map's verdict must agree with the research: if research turned the story, the map says so in its one line of why.

A story already past the map: run both once before its next draft is shown to Adrian, and fix the draft from them.
