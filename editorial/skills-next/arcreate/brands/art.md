## Who comes through this door

- The peer, never a follower: "yeah, that's right, brother." Someone looking for more meaning, who brings the art into their life because the connection came first. [plan]
- At this door: the maker, the considered collector, the builder with a soul. [plan]
- They come for presence and connection, not a product: "The art is not the point. The connection between people is the point." [About]
- No knowledge needed to arrive: "There is no need to understand what you are looking at." [Home] [UL]

## Voice

- Adrian in the first person. "I create art and spaces of presence and connection." [About]
- Peer with earned depth: the years, studios and process show in the piece, never from above. [plan]
- Taste stated flat, no hedging; sentences that build and layer. [voice]
- Material first, metaphor light: name the wood, the layers, the light, the stones before any feeling. [body]
- Quiet over loud. The work carries itself. [body]

## Words

- **Use:** presence, connection, creation, formless into form, multidimensional, cocreation, the Universal Language. [About] [UL] [How I Create]
- **Use, his quality words:** poignant, sharp, precise, cohesive, tasteful, effortless. [voice]
- **Avoid:** em dashes, emoji, "very" as filler, hedging openers, corporate tone. [voice] [Art.md]
- **Spiritual words are his and allowed:** ceremony, sacred, communion, presence. The rule is guidance, not a ban: steer away from the fluffy ones. Nothing has to be on a journey. [Adrian 3 Nov]
- **Avoid:** fluff and mysticism stacking. [Adrian 3 Nov] [body]

## Materials

- Layered Indonesian wood, each layer a separate piece, painted. [UL]
- Gemstones and crystals set where the spaces meet. [UL] [About]
- Laser cutting, airbrush, lapidary, 3D printing, digital design. [About] [How I Create]
- Light: LEDs and projection mapping. [About]
- "The geometry is exact. The laser is precise. But we humans embrace the splatter, the imperfect symmetry." [Home]
- Never bronze. The body-of-work file's "wood, bronze, light" is wrong; the work is not bronze. [Adrian 3 Nov]
