## Who comes through this door

- The peer, never a follower: "yeah, that's right, brother." Someone strengthening values in themselves, who shares the work with family and friends. [plan]
- At this door: the traveller of craft cultures, the brother, the quiet saver. [plan]
- What they come for, in his words: "By expressing what is true for me, perhaps others feel permission to do the same." [About]

## Voice

- Adrian in the first person. "Creating is how I grow." [About]
- The spiritual layer is authenticity: his actual life and discoveries, no keywords, no holding back what he has to give. [plan]
- Peer with earned depth: what he learned while writing is part of the story. [plan]
- Sentences that build and layer; "but" refines, never negates; taste stated flat. [voice]
- "Ideally" frames a wish; "I wonder if" is real thinking aloud. [voice]
- Reflection is finished work. A piece that never publishes has still done its job. [plan]

## Words

- **Use:** his quality words: poignant, sharp, precise, cohesive, tasteful, effortless. [voice]
- **Use:** presence, connection, creation, communion, formless into form. [About]
- **Avoid:** em dashes, emoji, hedging openers, "very" as filler, jargon. [voice]
- Spiritual words are his and allowed. Steer away from the fluffy ones. Nothing has to be on a journey. [Adrian 3 Nov]
