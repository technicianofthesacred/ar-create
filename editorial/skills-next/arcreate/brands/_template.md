# [Brand]

Built from Adrian's answers to five questions, asked once, the first time a recording for this brand arrives. Never guess a brand's voice. Ask them in chat, plainly, one message:

1. What is this brand about, in a sentence, and what kind of conversations will come in for it?
2. Who speaks for it: you in the first person, the brand, or someone else?
3. Where does it publish, and where should someone go after seeing a post?
4. Any words, topics or looks it must never use?
5. Anything it must stay apart from?

Then fill this file in his words and save it as `brands/<brand>.md`.
