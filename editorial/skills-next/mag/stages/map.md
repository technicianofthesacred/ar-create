# Map and plans: deciding what the story is

Read SYSTEM.md, "## 2a: The idea map (MAP)" and "## 2: Guidance — three plans (PLANS)", and the map's shape in `MAG/Workflow/2-Guidance/MAP TEMPLATE.md`. TEMPLATE.md section 5 says how many pieces a story can be.

## SRC, no map yet

0. **Research first** ([research.md](research.md)), if the story has no RESEARCH file. The map is chosen with its verdicts in hand.
1. **The analysis first.** Read the whole transcript, the story's newest draft and its NOTES if any, and write every deeper lesson, teaching and human moment into `MAG/Workflow/3-Develop/NOTES - [Title].md`. Adrian never reads NOTES; it is for the writer.
2. **Then his map**, `MAG/Workflow/2-Guidance/MAP - [Title].md`, shaped by the map template: one piece or several, with why; two or three directions, best fit first, each two or three sentences (what the story becomes, how it shapes the rest, how well it fits), then five or more titles. Short enough to decide in a minute. Adrian, 2026-10-01: "If you put too much I will not make the critical decisions."

Stop and show the map as a reading-page link ([preview.md](preview.md)). Ask him to pick, or say "defaults".

## Adrian has picked

Write his decision into the map under "Adrian's decisions", dated today, in his words where he gave them. Never overwrite an earlier decision. The live board reads a dated line there as the pick. Then make one plan per piece, `PLANS - [Title].md`.

Stop and show the plans.
