## Voice

- Literature, not a chat, a reference or a lesson. Plain English, chosen words, one thought per sentence. [brief]
- "Simple can be one of the most poetic if you pick your words correctly." [brief]
- Speak to you, always. Never "a person", "the reader", "this card", "the deck". [brief]
- Describe the state; never assign a life. No verdicts, no accusation. [brief]
- The oracle reflects a state; it does not issue an identity. [Oracle.md]
- Different people can speak here, and Adrian shows up here too. The deck is his creation, and it compiles many systems and people. [Adrian 3 Nov]
- The card prose itself is the deck speaking. [brief]

## Words

- **Use:** the card's name, which is Adrian's artwork title, never the hexagram's name. [brief]
- **Use:** words every culture has: a wave, fire, rain, the ground, a hand, a table; "call", not "ring". [brief]
- **Avoid:** em dashes, italics, sentences that open with "And", two "and"s in one sentence. [index] [brief]
- **Avoid:** horoscope tone, "ancient wisdom", "discover your true self", "the cards say", fixed openers like "This card invites you to". [Oracle.md]
- **Avoid:** names of any reader, translator, teacher or book; "the tradition says". [brief]
- **Avoid:** system words in prose (shadow, gift, siddhi, defined, open) outside their own sections. [brief]
- Richard Rudd is named only where a piece actually references his work, never on a card. [Adrian 3 Nov] [brief] [UL]
