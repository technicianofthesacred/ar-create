AR Create — three directions

Prototypes with invented content. Nothing here saves, runs AI or publishes.

A — The Document

The piece is the screen. Sections come to the text as sheets; the margin carries notes, decisions and sources.

B — The Studio

Organised by subject. One origin, its current understanding, and every expression that grows from it, on one board.

C — The Workbench

Organised by resuming. Pick up where you left off, see the next moves across everything, pick up a tool.

How they differ

The task: resume, add your own thought, correct a line in Source, return to the same spot, move carousel slide 3 to first, preview as a reader. The design review's suggestion: B's structure, with A's writing screen inside a piece and C's resume card on B's home.
