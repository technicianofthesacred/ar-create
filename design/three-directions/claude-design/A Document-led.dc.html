From the builder's notes

OpeningThe last piece, scrolled to the paragraph you left, with an inline "Left off here — next: tighten the ending" marker; the margin's first line says what changed while you were away.
SwitchingProject name or ⌘K opens a finder with left-off lines; sibling tabs for pieces from one origin; a slim rail with the outline above the twelve sections. Write and Read are modes; the rest open as sheets.
Best atLong writing and revision. The text is never more than one Esc away and "where I left off" is a place in the text.
CostsGather and Develop work lives in sheets, not a home of its own; a project with no piece has nothing to land on; thin overview; long margins at real volume; sheets hide the document.
To buildWrite becomes the resting state with a stored position; the 12-step sidebar collapses into the rail; the inspector becomes a permanent three-tab margin with note-to-passage anchors; carousel strip; media reverse index.

Critique in one paragraph

A is the best writing room of the three: the margin, Your words, protected-phrase checks and Focus make Write feel like a good editor's desk, and "left off" is a place in the text rather than a status card. It pays for that everywhere else. Projects are one click away but never in view, the carousel is a tall strip you scroll through rather than a sequence you see whole, and intake or research days happen in a sheet over a dimmed document. The fixes keep the document as home and give the other work enough room.

Top three fixes, as variants

Proposals only. The prototype file is not changed; these sketches show the structural change in the same look.

Fix 1 · must-have 8

Carousel opens as a contact sheet

A tall strip shows one slide at a time, so pacing and repetition across the six slides can't be judged at a glance.

Open the carousel as a grid of all slides at thumbnail size, text editable in place, drag to reorder between cells. The current strip stays as a "Read through" toggle for checking slide by slide.

Fix 2 · must-have 2 · 4

Projects in view, without leaving the document

Switching is one click, but knowing which of six projects needs you means opening the finder or Home.

Put a short "Projects" block at the top of the rail, above the outline: each active project on one line with its left-off line and a dot when something waits on you. The outline of the current piece follows below. ⌘K stays for search.

Fix 3 · must-have 5 · 6

Gather and Develop can take the screen beside the paragraph

Material, Source and Research open over a dimmed document. On a day of sorting or correcting, the sheet is cramped and the paragraph you came from is hidden.

Give each sheet a "Work here" width: the sheet takes most of the screen and the document shrinks to a narrow column on the right, still showing the paragraph you came from. A Source line can then be read against the sentence that quotes it.
