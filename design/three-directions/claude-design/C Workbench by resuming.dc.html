Critique in one paragraph

C is the strongest answer to "where was I" and "which project": the bench is inviting, specific and honest about who did what, and every next move lands on the exact line or slide. Its weakness is the right-hand understanding column. It is useful, but it is open on every tool, it competes with Write's own editorial margin, it splits Adrian's own words between two boxes, and under All projects it shows one project while the work shows another. Fix the column and C needs very little else.

Top three fixes, as variants

Proposals only. The prototype file is not changed; these sketches show the structural change in the same look.

Fix 1 · must-have 13 · 2

The column follows the scope and starts closed under All

Under All projects the Material screen lists every project while the column stays on Porcelain and Tea, the project you were last working in. The screen and the column give two answers to "where am I".

When the scope is All, close the column and put a one-line "Understanding: pick a project" link in its place. It opens only when a single project is in scope or an item is selected, and its header names that project. On phone it never opens by itself.

Fix 3 · must-have 3 · 12

One home for Adrian's own words

"Your thoughts — Adrian" in the column and "What I want this to say — Adrian" in Direction look like two records. Adrian has to guess which one agents read and which one carries forward.

Make them one record, "Your words", with dated entries. Direction shows it large at the top; the column shows the latest two entries and a link to the rest. Both say "only you write here". A thought added anywhere with T lands in the same list.
