From the builder's notes

OpeningStudio home: Pick up where you left off, Waiting on you, Since you left (Claude and Codex, with times), and a tile per subject with miniatures of its pieces.
SwitchingA permanent subject list with search and filters (All, Active, Waiting on me, Touched by Claude) and a state line per subject; the twelve sections as a tab row inside a subject, with a piece picker on piece-level sections.
Best atOne origin, many outputs as the structure itself; carry-forward chips on the board and an "inherits" column beside every piece; juggling many projects.
CostsTwo levels of navigation; a one-off post still needs a subject; the board needs ~1240px; Library and Collections fold into each subject; a 13-item tab row.
To buildSidebar becomes the subject list; steps move to a tab row; new Board and Studio home screens; inspector becomes the inherits column; per-subject state line, left-off record and a waiting-on-you query.

Critique in one paragraph

B gives the clearest picture of the product itself: a subject board makes Origins → Understanding → Pieces visible, carry-forward travels as chips into every piece, and the subject list with "Waiting on me" makes six projects easy to hold. The carousel and Media are the best of the three. It loses most on chrome: a breadcrumb, a 13-tab row, a piece picker and a toolbar stack above the text, the tab row runs out of room at 1280px, and anything without a subject has nowhere to go.

Fix 2 · must-have 13

The tab row fits by collapsing the list inside a piece

Thirteen tabs plus the subject list plus the inherits column fit at 1440 but not at 1280, where Share and Outputs fall off the edge.

When a piece opens, the subject list collapses to its initials rail automatically (one click restores it). The tab row shows the four group names with only the current group expanded, so it never scrolls sideways.

Fix 3 · must-have 1 · 11

Work can start without a subject

The brief says work can start anywhere. In B a quick post or a stray idea must first become a subject.

Add "Loose pieces" at the top of the subject list: pieces and ideas with no origin yet. Each has "Give it a subject" to move it onto a board later, carrying its decisions with it.
