Three ways to arrange the same workspace

The installed app works, but it does not look or move like the design you chose (Full A / Tree). These are three different answers to one question: what should you see first, and how do you get between projects and sections? All three use the same records, the same twelve sections and the same dark look. Only the structure changes.

The three directions

Document-led

The piece you are writing is the screen. Sections come to the text as sheets; the margin carries notes, decisions and sources.

The 13 things every direction must make visible

  1. 01Where am I, what can I do, without a manual.
  2. 02Many active projects: easy to find and switch.
  3. 03An obvious place for your own thoughts and words.
  4. 04Where I left off, and the current understanding.
  5. 05Material is intake; Source is understanding what was said.
  6. 06Research: inspect evidence, mark uncertainty, discuss one point.
  7. 07Write feels like a document; margin notes highlight their passage; Focus hides them.
  8. 08Carousel edited as a whole sequence.
  9. 09Media shows where each photo or video is used.
  10. 10Arrange, Audience and Export are three different jobs.
  11. 11One origin, several independently edited outputs.
  12. 12Decisions, protected phrases, corrections and rejected angles carry forward.
  13. 13No stacked headers, repeated notices, unexplained buttons or extra sidebars.

Each direction page shows the live prototype, its own tradeoffs, a critique against these 13, and three proposed fixes drawn as variants. Prototypes are built by other sessions; this canvas does not edit them. All content is invented.
