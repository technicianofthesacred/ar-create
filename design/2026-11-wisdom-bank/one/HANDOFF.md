## What is drawn

One surface that is dragged and pinched (wheel, and Ctrl with the wheel, on a computer). There are no pages, no sheets and no Open button.

- **From far away, everything together.** All 36 works sit on the one surface. They are not filed by subject. Each sits under the big idea it shares most with its neighbours ("You are already whole", "Letting the mind settle", "Some things only time can do", "What you repeat becomes you", "Rest is part of the work", "The material has a say", "Someone shows you"), so a tea article, a potter's notebook and a garden book lie side by side. The big ideas and the work titles stay readable however far out he is.
- **A work is kept whole, in one wide column.** Its parts in their order and every topic as a line of plain words. The book of a hundred topics is laid as nine parts, each a full-width column at reading size. On a phone, at reading size a column fills the screen and settles into place when he lets go.
- **A topic expands where it sits.** One tap: its one full sentence. "Go deeper": about three paragraphs. Again: the full reading in his order, with the passages on their own surface and the source. Pinching in past reading size on a topic also takes it one step deeper. Tapping its name folds it; everything below simply moves down and back.
- **A topic is a door.** Expanded, it lists every other work that holds it, each with a short straight line mark, and tapping one carries him across to that work with the same topic expanded there. Straight lines are drawn on the surface from the topic he is on to each of its other places, in one colour, and only for that topic.
- **Subjects are lights, not boxes.** "Light" (bottom left) lights a subject, a kind (Idea, Practice, Step, Belief) or what resonates most, and dims the rest without removing or moving anything. A work can carry several subjects: lighting Tea also lights "Water for tea" under "Letting the mind settle" and "The cracked cup" under "You are already whole".
- **Voice and expressing, from wherever he is.** One small floating microphone. It says what he is on, takes what he says (typed here, since a published page cannot use the microphone), and either keeps it as his note on that thing or gathers what the bank holds on it by lighting every matching topic across the surface. All his notes are listed in the same place and each leads back to where it was said. A note shows inside its topic, dated and outlined as his.
- **His marks.** Resonance (five dots) and Met it, Understand it, Practising it, Can teach it, inside each expanded topic. Resonance sets how strong a topic's dot is.
- The line at the top says where he is and goes back out.

- **Written by hand:** the book "The Long Apprenticeship" (nine parts, a hundred topics, a plain name and one full sentence each); the talk "The ground is already whole" (nine topics) and the tea article (five steps and a belief), carried from the first mock-ups. The three-paragraph reading exists for four topics ("You are already whole", "Some things only time can do", "Rest is part of the work", "Using 'it is all perfect' to avoid pain"). The full reading exists for one ("You are already whole").
- **Titles only:** the other 33 works. Each shows the topics it shares with the written works as words, the rest as a field of dots with a count, and says they are not written in this sample.

## Not done, and what breaks first

- **Real size is not here.** This is 36 works and 804 topics, drawn as ordinary page text. That is what makes the reading comfortable, and it is also the limit: a few hundred works could be laid out this way, but 2,000 works and 20,000 topics cannot all be live text at once. The real thing must draw only what is near the screen and stand in a picture for the rest. Not measured.
- **Placement is by hand.** Which big idea a work sits under was chosen by me for this sample. A real bank needs a rule that places new works near what they share and never moves what he already knows.
- **A work sits in one place.** "The Long Apprenticeship" touches almost everything and is shown once. Its ties are in its topics, not drawn until he is on one.
- **Gathering is by matching words**, not meaning, and stops at lighting the topics. Ordering them and making a piece are not drawn.
- The lines on the surface cross over text when the two places are far apart.
- Only one topic goes all the way down.

## Unsure of

- Whether seven big ideas as the landmarks reads as "all together" to him or as categories under another name. They are topics from the bank itself, and each opens like any topic.
- Whether dragging a column up and down at reading size feels like reading or like handling a canvas. On a phone it locks to up-and-down once he starts that way.
- Whether pinching in to go deeper will be found without being told. "Go deeper" does the same.
