Three directions for a noticeably better interface

Mock-ups only. Nothing in the app has changed. Each direction has Home, a subject page (full, and brand new) and Write (with a source beside the words, and in plain focus). All three tell the same invented story so they can be compared.

  1. A. The boardToday's frame, finished: the sidebar becomes a path, Home becomes a desk of subject cards, and the Subject board is the centre of each subject.
  2. B. The pathNo sidebar. Every subject is a line with four stations. Home lays the lines side by side; a subject page is its line turned upright with the work at each station.
  3. C. The work itselfNo bars at all. Subjects are covers, pieces are shown as large as they will look, and a floating dock carries the few controls.
All names, words and pictures are invented. Photographs and slides are drawn shapes.
