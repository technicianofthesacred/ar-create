Why does water change the taste of tea?

Current understanding
Minerals change how flavour leaves the leaf. Moderate hardness tends to give body; very soft water can taste thin. How this applies to Lin Yun’s oolong is not established.
Point 1 predates your correction. Waiting for your review; not rewritten.

1 · Limestone makes hard water Supported

Rain picks up carbon dioxide, passes through limestone and dissolves calcium carbonate, so limestone springs usually give hard water.

Lu Yu ranks mountain spring water first (retold in The Book of Tea). Modern guides warn very soft water under-extracts.
