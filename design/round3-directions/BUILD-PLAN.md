# Chosen design — build plan

Decision (Adrian, 20 October 2026): **"go with Full A, the Tree"**.
Prototype: `Full-A-Tree.dc.html` on the canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/WCibjsYNREMrVdaToKWGpR (shared source in `full-src/`).
Superseded: the earlier "B + A's pages + C's Where used" pick and the Chosen / Chosen 2 / Layout 1–3 sketches. They stay on the canvas as history.

## What Full A is

- **Sidebar:** Home, then the four activities (Gather, Develop, Make, Deliver) with all twelve steps inside them, each showing only what genuinely needs you. Below: Library, Topics, Projects, and "Everything that's here". It closes only with its own arrow and becomes a slim rail.
- **Working on switch** at the top: everything, one project or one topic. Every list follows it, so you can lock into one kind of work across everything (all transcripts, all research) or stay inside one project.
- **Middle list:** what's waiting in that step, across the chosen scope.
- **Work area:** the full working screen for that step. A piece (article, carousel, post, listing, script) shows its own step path in the original order with one Next line. The magazine story keeps Map, Plan, Draft, Checks, Edit passes, Photos, Publish, Feedback.
- **Docked panel** on request: Find in Library, Where it's used, History.
- **Pinned footer:** where you are, save status, text size; it turns red with Try again when a save fails.

## Rules for every step of the build

Press the existing owners (see the handler map on the canvas Start-here page). No new store, history system or dependency. Never on port 8841. Keep the twelve step identities and the C3 hooks (`.workflowStep`, `OWN`, `TOOLS`, `STEPS`). No sideways scrolling. Drag and drop for reordering. Plain words from the first-time review. Each step is its own branch and pull request, landed before the next starts.

## Order (lowest risk first)

1. **Sidebar with activities and steps** — regroup today's checkpoint rail into the four activities with honest counts; add the collapse arrow and rail. Look modules only, like C3. About a day.
2. **Working on switch and step lists** — a scope picker and the middle list per step, reading the owners that already exist (outputs overview, source sessions, saved research, direction). About two days.
3. **Piece step paths and the Next line** — per-kind paths over the existing Write/Read/Check/Media/Arrange/Audience/Share screens; the magazine path for stories. About a day.
4. **Material: keep or remove, and sorting into topics** — intake review and the needs-you sorter; topics as links, one item in many. Needs standalone Collections installed (prepared). About three days after that.
5. **Docked panel: Library, Where it's used, History** — Where it's used combines media uses, source-turn references and topic links; History reads existing receipts. Two to three days; full history needs the accepted, unbuilt work.
6. **Home, plain words, states, checklist page** — purpose line, Needs you, What do you want to do, every state, the "Everything that's here" page. About a day.

Steps 1 to 3 and 6 can start now. Steps 4 and 5 wait on prepared pieces being installed.
