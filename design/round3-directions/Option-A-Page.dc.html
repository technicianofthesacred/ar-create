Why does water change the taste of tea?

Current understanding
Minerals change how flavour leaves the leaf. Moderate hardness tends to give body; very soft water can taste thin; very hard water can flatten aroma. How this applies to Lin Yun’s oolong is not established.

1 · Limestone makes hard water Supported

Rain picks up carbon dioxide, passes through limestone and dissolves calcium carbonate. Water from limestone springs is therefore usually hard.

2 · Minerals and extraction Qualified

Calcium and magnesium bind with flavour compounds; too few and the cup goes thin, too many and it flattens. Most measured work is on black and green tea. Oolong evidence is thin.
