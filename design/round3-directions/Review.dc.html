Where a first-time user gets lost, and the fixes

Two fresh reviewers read every word of the Chosen prototype: one for wording, one for how the parts connect and whether everything can be reached. Below: the three choices that are yours, then what I will change. Nothing is changed yet.

Why you didn't know where to start

  • Nothing says what AR Create is for.
  • The biggest card says “Continue”, but a newcomer has nothing to continue.
  • The sidebar talks first, in words the page never explains: Pinned, Topics, Things I’m making.
  • The same thing has up to four names: topic, collection, inquiry and concept all appear.
  • Inside a piece, most of the twelve steps are hidden behind “More”, and Read and Check look identical to Write.

Proposed first line on Home, shown until you dismiss it

AR Create turns your recordings, notes, photos and reading into articles, carousels and posts. Start with one thought. You can add the rest later.

Three choices for you needs you

My pick is outlined in gold. Say “your picks” to accept all three, or name a different one.

For the thing you make: “Piece”

Short, already in your docs, and it stops being confusing once Home defines it in one line. Lists name the kind anyway: Article, Carousel.

Or: “Work”

The reviewer’s pick. Reads naturally in “Work in Water first”, but “work” is also a verb, so buttons get ambiguous.

Or: no umbrella word

Always say Article, Carousel, Post. Cleanest on screen, but lists of mixed kinds need a heading.

3 · How the steps show inside a piece

Always show this piece’s own steps

One slim row under the title. An article shows Write, Read, Check, Photos, Share. A carousel shows Arrange, Photos, Audience, Share. The rest of the twelve stay one click away. The “uses” list stays under More.

Keep all steps behind More

Calmest screen, but the reviewer found that 7 of 12 steps can only be reached through More, which is why moving around felt hidden.

What I will fix, whatever you choose

How things connect

  1. Home starts with the one-line purpose and “Start something new”. “Pick up where you stopped” shows only when there is something to pick up.
  2. Read and Check get their own screens: Read is a clean page with no side notes; Check is the list of things to fix, each one jumping to its sentence.
  3. Opening Photos from the article stays with the article; it no longer jumps to the carousel.
  4. The topic page and its research page become one page: what we know, the evidence, open questions, and what uses it.
  5. Every link works both ways: a piece shows its topic, a quote shows its recording, a slide shows its photo, a decision shows the pieces it applies to.
  6. “Library” means one thing: the page. The quick search panel becomes “Find”.
  7. Every button says where it goes. “Choose”, “Look”, “Open”, “Carry into Direction” and “Rerun checks” get plain labels, and each “Open” opens the thing it sits next to.
  8. Buttons that do nothing in this demo are shown as greyed out with “Not available yet”, instead of looking clickable.

Usable by everyone

  1. Arrows and “+” in the sidebar stay faint but always visible, so they work on touch screens and with a keyboard.
  2. Folders open from the keyboard; the slide order can be changed with keys as well as by dragging.
  3. The marked sentence and the slides become real buttons that the keyboard and screen readers reach.
  4. Every coloured dot also has words: “needs you”, “finished”.
  5. All controls at least 36 pixels tall, 44 on phones; the smallest 10px text goes up to 12px.
  6. Control outlines get stronger so buttons are visible against the dark page; dimmed map cards stay readable.
  7. On a phone the sidebar starts closed behind a Menu button, and side notes appear under the paragraph they belong to.
  8. Text contrast already passes everywhere: 6.4 to 1 in dark, 5.8 to 1 in light.
